# 阅读高频词汇

## 学术类词汇
- hypothesis, methodology, empirical, theoretical, findings
- correlation, causation, variable, data, statistics
- phenomenon, implication, interpretation, analysis, synthesis

## 同义替换高频词
- significant → considerable/substantial/notable
- increase → rise/grow/expand/escalate
- decrease → decline/diminish/reduce/dwindle
- show → demonstrate/illustrate/reveal/indicate
- important → crucial/vital/essential/fundamental

## 逻辑关系词
- 因果: consequently, therefore, hence, thereby, as a result
- 转折: however, nevertheless, conversely, on the contrary
- 递进: furthermore, moreover, additionally, in addition
- 举例: for instance, such as, namely, specifically

## 学科分类词汇
### 自然科学
- evolution, species, ecosystem, organism, adaptation

### 社会科学
- civilization, urbanization, industrialization, migration

### 心理学
- cognitive, perception, stimulus, behavior, consciousness
