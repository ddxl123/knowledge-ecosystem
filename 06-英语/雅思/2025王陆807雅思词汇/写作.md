# 写作场景词汇

## 教育话题
- curriculum, pedagogy, vocational, academic, literacy
- compulsory, extracurricular, discipline, assessment

## 科技话题
- innovation, automation, artificial intelligence, digital
- breakthrough, revolutionize, cutting-edge, obsolete

## 环境话题
- emission, renewable, sustainable, conservation, pollution
- deforestation, biodiversity, carbon footprint, ecosystem

## 社会话题
- inequality, demographic, urbanization, welfare, poverty
- discrimination, globalization, infrastructure, governance

## 高分连接词
- Firstly/Secondly/Finally → In the first place/Subsequently/Lastly
- In my opinion → From my perspective/As far as I am concerned
- For example → For instance/To illustrate/A case in point is
- In conclusion → To sum up/In summary/To conclude/All in all
