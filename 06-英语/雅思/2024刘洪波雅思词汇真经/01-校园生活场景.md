| enrollment | n. | 注册 | registration |

| postgraduate | n. | 研究生 | graduate student |

## 考点搭配
- enroll in a course
- submit an assignment
- meet the deadline
- academic performance

## 易错点
1. ⚠️ tuition 英式指"教学"，美式指"学费"
2. ⚠️ faculty 英式指"院系"，美式指"教员"
3. ⚠️ seminar vs lecture
4. ⚠️ curriculum 复数形式是 curricula
5. ⚠️ plagiarism 动词是 plagiarize
