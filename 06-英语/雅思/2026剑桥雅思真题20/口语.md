# 剑桥雅思20 口语

## Part 1 - 日常话题
### Topic 1: Technology
- Do you use any AI tools in your daily life?
- How has technology changed the way you communicate?
- Do you think people spend too much time on their phones?

### Topic 2: Home
- What is your favorite room in your home?
- Would you like to move to a different place in the future?
- Do you prefer living in a house or an apartment?

### Topic 3: Health
- How do you keep healthy?
- Do you think people today are more health-conscious than before?
- What changes would you make to improve your lifestyle?

## Part 2 - 话题卡
### Card 1: 描述一次使用科技的经历
- What the technology was
- When you used it
- How you used it
- How you felt about it

### Card 2: 描述一个你想参观的地方
- Where it is
- What it is famous for
- How you learned about it
- Why you want to visit it

### Card 3: 描述一个对你影响很大的人
- Who the person is
- How you know this person
- What they did that influenced you
- How they changed your thinking

### Card 4: 描述一个环保行动
- What the action was
- When you did it
- How you did it
- Why you think it was important

## Part 3 - 深入讨论
### 科技与社会
- How do you think AI will change education in the future?
- Should governments regulate the use of artificial intelligence?
- What are the potential risks of relying too heavily on technology?

### 环境与可持续发展
- What role should individuals play in protecting the environment?
- Do you think renewable energy can completely replace fossil fuels?
- How can governments encourage people to adopt more sustainable lifestyles?

### 工作与生活
- How has remote work affected people's work-life balance?
- Do you think the traditional 9-to-5 work model will disappear?
- What skills will be most important for future workers?

## 口语技巧
1. Part 1 回答2-3句话即可
2. Part 2 充分利用1分钟准备时间
3. Part 3 展示深度思考和论证能力
4. 使用连接词保持流利度
5. 适当使用高级词汇和句型
