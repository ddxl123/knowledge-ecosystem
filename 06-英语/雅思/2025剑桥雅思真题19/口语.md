# 剑桥雅思19 口语

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
雅思口语分为Part 1（日常问答）、Part 2（个人陈述）、Part 3（深入讨论），考查考生的口语表达能力。

### 知识结构
- Part 1 - 日常话题（家庭、工作、兴趣、家乡等）
- Part 2 - 话题卡（人物、地点、物品、经历）
- Part 3 - 深入讨论（社会问题、抽象话题）

### 待收集原子知识点
- 流利度与连贯性：减少停顿和重复，使用连接词保持语流
- 词汇丰富度：使用多样化词汇，避免重复，适当使用习语和搭配
- 语法准确性：正确使用多种时态和句型，减少语法错误
- 发音：清晰的发音、适当的语调和重音
- 话题展开：围绕话题展开论述，提供细节、例子和原因

## Test 1
### Part 1
- Do you like reading?
- What kind of books do you prefer?
- Do you read more now than before?

### Part 2
- Describe a skill you would like to learn
- What the skill is / Why you want to learn it / How you plan to learn it / How it would benefit you

### Part 3
- Why do some people learn new skills more easily?
- Is it better to learn skills formally or informally?
- How has technology changed the way people learn skills?

## Test 2
### Part 1
- Do you enjoy cooking?
- What's your favorite dish to make?
- Do you think cooking is an important skill?

### Part 2
- Describe a memorable trip you took
- Where you went / Who you went with / What you did / Why it was memorable

### Part 3
- Why do people like to travel?
- How has travel changed over the decades?
- Is it important to travel to understand other cultures?

## 评分标准
- Fluency & Coherence: 25%
- Lexical Resource: 25%
- Grammatical Range & Accuracy: 25%
- Pronunciation: 25%
