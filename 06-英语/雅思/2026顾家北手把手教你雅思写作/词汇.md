# 高分写作词汇

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
写作词汇是雅思写作提分的关键，包括话题词汇、论证词汇和高级替换词汇。

### 知识结构
- 话题词汇 - 教育类、科技类、环境类、社会类
- 论证连接词 - 因果、转折、递进、举例、总结
- 高级替换表 - 常见词 - 高级词

### 待收集原子知识点
- 话题词汇：按教育、科技、环境等话题分类的核心词汇
- 论证词汇：表达因果、转折、递进等逻辑关系的连接词
- 高级替换：将常见词汇替换为更高级的表达
- 搭配模式：掌握词汇的常见搭配，避免中式英语

## 话题词汇分类
### 教育类
| 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| curriculum | 课程体系 | The curriculum should be updated regularly. |
| pedagogical | 教学法的 | Pedagogical approaches vary across cultures. |
| vocational | 职业的 | Vocational training prepares students for specific careers. |
| holistic | 全面的 | A holistic approach to education is essential. |

### 科技类
| 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| automation | 自动化 | Automation may lead to job displacement. |
| innovation | 创新 | Technological innovation drives economic growth. |
| surveillance | 监控 | Surveillance technology raises privacy concerns. |
| digital literacy | 数字素养 | Digital literacy is a crucial skill in the 21st century. |

### 环境类
| 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| sustainability | 可持续性 | Environmental sustainability should be a priority. |
| renewable | 可再生的 | Renewable energy sources are becoming more affordable. |
| conservation | 保护 | Wildlife conservation requires collective effort. |
| carbon-neutral | 碳中和的 | Many companies aim to become carbon-neutral by 2050. |

### 社会类
| 词汇 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| inequality | 不平等 | Social inequality remains a pressing issue. |
| urbanization | 城市化 | Rapid urbanization brings both opportunities and challenges. |
| demographic | 人口统计的 | Demographic shifts affect the labor market. |
| welfare | 福利 | The welfare system needs reform. |

## 2026年更新要点
- 新增了远程工作（remote work）相关词汇
- 增加了心理健康（mental health）话题词汇
- 更新了社交媒体（social media）影响类表达
- 新增了代际差异（generational gap）相关词汇

## 连接词与过渡词
### 表递进
- furthermore / moreover / in addition / additionally

### 表转折
- however / nevertheless / nonetheless / conversely

### 表因果
- therefore / consequently / as a result / hence

### 表举例
- for instance / for example / to illustrate / specifically

### 表总结
- in conclusion / to sum up / overall / in summary
