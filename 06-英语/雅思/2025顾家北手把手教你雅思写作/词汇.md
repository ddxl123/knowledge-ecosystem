# 写作高分词汇

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
写作词汇是雅思写作提分的关键，包括话题词汇、论证词汇和高级替换词汇。

### 知识结构
- 话题词汇 - 教育类、科技类、环境类、社会类
- 论证连接词 - 因果、转折、递进、举例、总结
- 高级替换表 - 常见词 - 高级词

### 待收集原子知识点
- 话题词汇：按教育、科技、环境等话题分类的核心词汇
- 论证词汇：表达因果、转折、递进等逻辑关系的连接词
- 高级替换：将常见词汇替换为更高级的表达
- 搭配模式：掌握词汇的常见搭配，避免中式英语

## 同义替换词汇
### 表达"重要的"
- important → crucial/vital/significant/essential/paramount

### 表达"认为"
- think → believe/argue/maintain/contend/assert

### 表达"越来越多的"
- more and more → an increasing number of/a growing number of/a rising proportion of

### 表达"因为"
- because → due to/owing to/as a result of/on account of/attributed to

### 表达"然而"
- but → however/nevertheless/nonetheless/on the other hand/conversely

## 话题词汇
### 教育
- curriculum, syllabus, compulsory education, vocational training, academic performance

### 科技
- technological advancement, artificial intelligence, automation, digital literacy

### 环境
- carbon emissions, renewable energy, sustainable development, ecological balance

### 健康
- sedentary lifestyle, mental well-being, preventive measures, healthcare system

- play a pivotal role in
- have a profound impact on
- give rise to
- take...into consideration
