# 剑桥雅思18 口语

## Test 1
### Part 1
- Where do you live?
- Do you like your neighborhood?
- What do you usually do in your free time?

### Part 2
- Describe a person who has inspired you
- Who the person is / How you know them / What they did / Why they inspired you

### Part 3
- Do you think young people need role models?
- What qualities make someone a good role model?
- Has the concept of role models changed over time?

## Test 2
### Part 1
- Do you work or study?
- What do you enjoy about your work/studies?
- Do you prefer working alone or in a team?

### Part 2
- Describe a place you visited that you found surprisingly enjoyable
- Where it was / When you went / What you did there / Why it surprised you

### Part 3
- Why do people travel?
- How has tourism changed in recent years?
- Is it better to travel alone or with others?

## 备考建议
1. 录音练习，回听找问题
2. 准备话题素材库
3. 练习流利度而非背诵
4. 注意发音和语调变化
