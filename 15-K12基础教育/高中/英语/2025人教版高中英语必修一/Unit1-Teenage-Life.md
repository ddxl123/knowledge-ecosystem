## 语法：名词性从句（主语从句）

### 定义
在句中充当主语的从句叫主语从句。

### 引导词
1. **that：** 无意义，不充当成分（不可省略）
   - That he passed the exam surprised us all.
2. **whether：** 是否
   - Whether he will come is unknown.
3. **连接代词：** who, what, which, whoever, whatever等
   - What he said is true.
4. **连接副词：** when, where, how, why等
   - Where he went is still a mystery.

### it作形式主语
- It is + adj. + that...：It is important that we study hard.
- It is + n. + that...：It is a fact that the earth is round.
- It + v. + that...：It seems that he is happy.

### 注意事项
- 主语从句要用陈述语序
- that引导主语从句时不可省略
- 主句谓语动词通常用单数
