# Unit 1: My name's Gina

## 重点词汇
- name /neɪm/ 名字
- nice /naɪs/ 令人愉快的
- meet /miːt/ 遇见
- too /tuː/ 也；太
- your /jɔːr/ 你的；你们的
- his /hɪz/ 他的
- her /hɜːr/ 她的
- phone /fəʊn/ 电话
- number /ˈnʌmbər/ 数字；号码
- first name 名字
- last name 姓氏
- friend /frend/ 朋友
- China /ˈtʃaɪnə/ 中国
- middle /ˈmɪdl/ 中间的
- school /skuːl/ 学校

## 核心句型
1. — What's your name? — My name's Gina. / I'm Gina.
2. — What's his name? — His name's Eric. / He's Eric.
3. — What's her name? — Her name's Mary. / She's Mary.
4. — Nice to meet you! — Nice to meet you, too!
5. — What's your phone number? — It's 281-9176.

## 语法要点

### be动词的用法
| 主语 | be动词 | 缩写 |
|------|--------|------|
| I | am | I'm |
| he/she/it/单数 | is | he's/she's/it's |
| you/we/they/复数 | are | you're/we're/they're |

### 人称代词与物主代词
| 人称代词(主格) | 物主代词(形容词性) |
|---------------|-------------------|
| I | my |
| you | your |
| he | his |
| she | her |
| it | its |
| we | our |
| they | their |

## 课文要点
- 自我介绍的基本表达
- 中英文名字的区别（英文：first name + last name；中文：last name + first name）
- 电话号码的读法（逐个数字读出）

## 对话范例
A: Hello! What's your name?
B: My name's Linda. What's your name?
A: I'm Jim. Nice to meet you!
B: Nice to meet you, too!
A: What's your phone number?
B: It's 398-6149.
