# Unit 3: Is this your pencil?

## 核心句型
1. — Is this your pencil? — Yes, it is. It's mine. / No, it isn't. It's his.
2. — Is that your book? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. It's hers.
3. — Are these your books? — No, they aren't. They're his.
4. — What about this dictionary? — It's Helen's.
5. Thank you for your help. 谢谢你的帮助。
6. You're welcome. 不客气。

## 语法要点

### 一般疑问句（be动词引导）
- **结构：** Be动词 + 主语 + 其他？
- **肯定回答：** Yes, 主语 + be动词.
- **否定回答：** No, 主语 + be动词 + not.

### 名词性物主代词
| 形容词性物主代词 | 名词性物主代词 |
|-----------------|---------------|
| my | mine |
| your | yours |
| his | his |
| her | hers |
| its | its |
| our | ours |
| their | theirs |

**区别：** 形容词性物主代词后必须跟名词（my book），名词性物主代词单独使用（It's mine.）

### 名词所有格
- 单数名词加 's: Tom's book
- 以s结尾的复数名词加 ': the students' books
- 不以s结尾的复数名词加 's: children's books

## 对话范例
A: Excuse me, Grace. Is this your pencil?
B: Yes, thank you. And those are my erasers.
A: And Jane, is this your ruler?
C: No, it isn't. It's her ruler.
A: OK, and this is my book. And this is your dictionary, Jane.
C: Thank you!
