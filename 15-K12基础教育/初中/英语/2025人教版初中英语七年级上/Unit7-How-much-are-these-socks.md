## 核心句型
1. — How much is this T-shirt? — It's seven dollars.
2. — How much are these socks? — They're two dollars.
3. — Can I help you? — Yes, please. I need a sweater.
4. I'll take it/them. 我要买它/它们。
5. Here you are. 给你。

## 语法要点

### how much的用法
- **询问价格：** How much + is/are + 主语？
- **询问不可数名词的数量：** How much water do you need?
- 区别：How many + 可数名词复数

### 基数词（10-31）
| 10-19 | 20-31 |
|-------|-------|
| ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, eighteen, nineteen | twenty, twenty-one, ..., thirty, thirty-one |

### 购物常用表达
- Can I help you? / What can I do for you? 需要帮忙吗？
- I want/need... 我想要/需要……
- How much is/are...? ……多少钱？
- I'll take it/them. 我要买它/它们。
- Here you are. 给你。
- Thank you. / You're welcome.

### 形容词的用法
- 形容词放在名词前：a big hat, a red skirt
- 形容词放在be动词后：The hat is big. The skirt is red.

## 对话范例
A: Can I help you?
B: Yes, please. I need a sweater for school.
A: OK. What color do you want?
B: Blue.
A: How about this one?
B: It looks nice. How much is it?
A: Nine dollars.
B: I'll take it. How much are those yellow socks?
A: Two dollars for one pair and three dollars for two pairs.
B: Great! I'll take two pairs.
A: Here you are.
B: Thank you.
A: You're welcome.
