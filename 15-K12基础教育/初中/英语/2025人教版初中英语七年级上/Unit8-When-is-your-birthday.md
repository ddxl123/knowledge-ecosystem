## 核心句型
1. — When is your birthday? — My birthday is on May 2nd.
2. — When is his birthday? — His birthday is on January 17th.
3. — When is the school trip? — It's on September 15th.
4. Happy birthday! 生日快乐！
5. How old are you? 你多大了？

## 语法要点

### when引导的特殊疑问句
- **结构：** When + be动词 + 事件？
- **回答：** It's + 日期. / 事件 + is + on + 日期.

### 序数词（1st-31st）
| 基数词 | 序数词 | 缩写 |
|--------|--------|------|
| one | first | 1st |
| two | second | 2nd |
| three | third | 3rd |
| four | fourth | 4th |
| five | fifth | 5th |
| eight | eighth | 8th |
| nine | ninth | 9th |
| twelve | twelfth | 12th |
| twenty | twentieth | 20th |
| thirty | thirtieth | 30th |

**规则：** 一般在基数词后加-th，注意特殊变化（first, second, third, fifth, eighth, ninth, twelfth, twentieth）

### 日期的表达
- **写法：** 月 + 日（序数词）：May 2nd, September 15th
- **读法：** May (the) second, September (the) fifteenth
- **介词：** 具体日期前用on（on May 2nd），月份前用in（in May）

### 月份的首字母必须大写

## 对话范例
A: Hi, Alan. Happy birthday!
B: Thank you, Bill.
A: So, how old are you, Alan?
B: I'm twelve. How old are you?
A: I'm thirteen.
B: When is your birthday?
A: My birthday is in August.
B: Well, do you want to come to my birthday party?
A: Oh, yes. When is it?
B: At three this afternoon.
A: OK, great. See you!
