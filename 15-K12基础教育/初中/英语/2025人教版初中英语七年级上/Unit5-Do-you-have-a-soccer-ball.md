## 核心句型
1. — Do you have a soccer ball? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
2. — Does she have a tennis ball? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
3. — Do they have a basketball? — Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
4. Let's play basketball! 让我们打篮球吧！
5. That sounds good. 那听起来不错。
6. I don't have a soccer ball. 我没有足球。
7. He doesn't have a volleyball. 他没有排球。

## 语法要点

### have/has的用法
- **have：** 用于第一人称（I/we）、第二人称（you）和复数主语
- **has：** 用于第三人称单数（he/she/it/单数名词）

### 否定句
- I/You/We/They **don't have**...
- He/She/It **doesn't have**...
- 注意：doesn't后面用动词原形have

### 一般疑问句
- **Do** + I/you/we/they + have...?
- **Does** + he/she/it + have...?
- 注意：Does后面用动词原形have

### let的用法
- Let's = Let us + 动词原形
- Let me + 动词原形
- Let him/her/them + 动词原形

## 对话范例
A: Let's play soccer!
B: I don't have a soccer ball.
A: Well, let's play volleyball!
B: That sounds good. Do you have a volleyball?
A: No, I don't. Does your brother Alan have one?
B: Yes, he does. Let's go!
