## 核心句型
1. — What's your favorite subject? — My favorite subject is science.
2. — Why do you like science? — Because it's interesting.
3. — Who is your science teacher? — My science teacher is Mr. Wang.
4. — When is your math class? — It's on Monday and Friday.
5. I like Monday because I have P.E. and music.

## 语法要点

### what/why/who引导的特殊疑问句
- **What：** 询问事物 — What's your favorite subject?
- **Why：** 询问原因 — Why do you like math?
- **Who：** 询问人 — Who is your music teacher?

### because的用法
- **because + 句子：** 表示原因
- **注意：** because不能与so同时使用
- 错误：Because it's fun, so I like it.
- 正确：Because it's fun, I like it. / I like it because it's fun.

### 星期的表达
- 首字母必须大写
- 介词用on：on Monday, on Friday
- on weekdays 在工作日
- on weekends 在周末

## 对话范例
A: What's your favorite subject, David?
B: My favorite subject is Chinese.
A: Why do you like Chinese?
B: Because it's fun. What's your favorite subject?
A: Hmm. I like P.E.
B: Why do you like P.E.?
A: Because it's exciting. And our P.E. teacher is very funny.
B: Who is your P.E. teacher?
A: Mr. Li. He always plays games with us.
B: That sounds great!
