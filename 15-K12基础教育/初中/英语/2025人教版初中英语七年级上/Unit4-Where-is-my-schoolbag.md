## 核心句型
1. — Where's my schoolbag? — It's under the table.
2. — Where are my books? — They're on the sofa.
3. — Where's his pencil box? — It's in his schoolbag.
4. — Is the ruler on the bed? — No, it isn't. It's under the bed.
5. I think it's in your grandparents' room.
6. Come on, Jack! 快点，杰克！

## 语法要点

### where引导的特殊疑问句
- **结构：** Where + be动词 + 主语？
- **回答：** 主语 + be动词 + 介词短语（表示位置）

### 方位介词
| 介词 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| in | 在……里面 | The book is in the bag. |
| on | 在……上面 | The cup is on the table. |
| under | 在……下面 | The cat is under the bed. |
| behind | 在……后面 | The ball is behind the door. |
| next to | 在……旁边 | The chair is next to the desk. |
| between | 在……之间 | The bed is between the two tables. |

### 介词短语的用法
- 介词短语 = 介词 + 名词/代词
- 介词短语在句中作表语或状语
- 注意：介词后的人称代词用宾格（under it, not under its）

## 对话范例
A: Hey, Helen! Where are my books?
B: They're on the sofa.
A: How about my pencil box?
B: It's in your schoolbag.
A: OK. And where's my computer game?
B: Your computer game? It's under your bed.
A: Great! Where are my keys?
B: They're on the table.
