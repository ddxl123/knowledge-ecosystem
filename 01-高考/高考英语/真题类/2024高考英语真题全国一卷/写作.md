## 第一节 短文改错

### 原文（含10处错误）

Last summer, I went to Beijing with my parents. It was my first time to visit the capital city, so I was very exciting. On the first day, we visited the Forbidden City where is one of the most famous tourist attraction in China. The buildings were so beautifully that I took hundreds of photos. On the second day, we climb the Great Wall. It was tired but worth it. I also tried some local food, that were delicious. Beside visiting famous places, we also went shopping. I bought a lots of souvenirs for my friends. What unforgettable trip!

### 改错答案

1. **exciting → excited** （修饰人的感受用-ed形式）
2. **where → which** （定语从句中作主语用which不用where）
3. **attraction → attractions** （one of + 名词复数）
4. **beautifully → beautiful** （作表语用形容词）
5. **climb → climbed** （过去时态）
6. **tired → tiring** （修饰事物用-ing形式）
7. **that → which** （非限制性定语从句不用that）
8. **Beside → Besides** （beside=在旁边，besides=除了）
9. **a lots → a lot / lots** （a lots搭配错误）
10. **What → What a** （感叹句：What a/an + 形容词 + 可数名词单数！）

## 第二节 书面表达

### 题目
假定你是李华，你的英国朋友Chris来信说他最近在学习中文，但觉得很难。请你给他回信，内容包括：
1. 表示鼓励；
2. 提出学习建议；
3. 表达祝愿。

注意：1. 词数100左右；2. 可以适当增加细节，以使行文连贯。

### 参考范文

Dear Chris,

I'm glad to hear that you're learning Chinese! I understand it can be challenging, but don't worry — many people have successfully learned it, and I'm sure you will too.

Here are some suggestions that might help. First, try to learn characters by understanding their components rather than memorizing them randomly. This makes them much easier to remember. Second, practice speaking as much as possible. You could find a language partner online or watch Chinese TV shows with subtitles. Third, don't be afraid of making mistakes. They are a natural part of learning.

I'd also be happy to practice with you whenever you want. We could have weekly video calls where we speak only in Chinese for 30 minutes.

Keep going, and you'll be surprised how quickly you improve!

Best wishes,
Li Hua

### 写作要点分析

**结构:** 三段式（开头回应+中间建议+结尾鼓励）

**亮点句型:**
- I understand it can be challenging...（表达理解与共情）
- ...by understanding their components rather than memorizing...（rather than对比）
- They are a natural part of learning.（定性错误的价值）
- ...you'll be surprised how quickly you improve.（鼓励性结语）

**高频词汇:**
- challenging (有挑战性的)
- rather than (而不是)
- language partner (语伴)
- a natural part of (……的自然组成部分)
