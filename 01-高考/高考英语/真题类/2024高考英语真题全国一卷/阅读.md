## A篇（应用文）

**话题:** 四个适合家庭的博物馆

**Passage:**

**The Science Discovery Museum**
Located in the heart of downtown, this museum offers hands-on exhibits for children aged 3-12. Open Tuesday-Sunday, 10 AM-5 PM. Admission: Adults $15, Children $8, Under 3 free.

**The History Center**
Explore 500 years of local history through interactive displays. Special weekend workshops for families. Open daily 9 AM-6 PM. Admission: Adults $12, Children $6, Family pass (2 adults + 2 children) $30.

**The Art Garden**
An outdoor museum featuring sculptures and paintings in a beautiful garden setting. Open Wednesday-Monday, 8 AM-8 PM. Free admission on the first Sunday of each month. Regular admission: $10 per person.

**The Children's Museum**
Designed specifically for kids under 8, with water play areas, building zones, and a mini supermarket. Open Monday-Saturday, 9:30 AM-4:30 PM. Admission: $12 per child (one adult free per child).

**第21题:** Which museum is best for a 5-year-old?
**答案:** The Children's Museum（专为8岁以下儿童设计）

**第22题:** How much does a family of 4 (2 adults, 2 children) pay at The History Center?
**答案:** $30（家庭通票价格）

**第23题:** When can you visit The Art Garden for free?
**答案:** On the first Sunday of each month

## B篇（记叙文）

**话题:** 一位退休教师的社区图书馆项目

**Passage:**

When Maria Gonzalez retired from teaching after 35 years, she didn't just sit back and relax. Instead, she noticed that many children in her neighborhood had nowhere to read after the local library closed due to budget cuts.

"I couldn't just watch these kids lose access to books," Maria said. She started by setting up a small bookshelf on her front porch with about 50 books she had collected over her teaching career. Within a week, the books were all borrowed.

Encouraged by the response, Maria launched "Books for All," a community library project. She reached out to local businesses and residents for book donations. Within six months, she had collected over 3,000 books and set up reading corners in three community centers.

The project didn't stop there. Maria organized weekly reading sessions where retired teachers and college students volunteered to read to younger children. She also created a mobile library that visited different neighborhoods every Saturday.

Today, "Books for All" serves over 500 families and has inspired similar projects in neighboring towns. Maria was awarded the Community Service Medal by the mayor last year.

**第24题:** Why did Maria start the project?
**答案:** The local library had closed and children had no place to read

**第25题:** How did Maria begin?
**答案:** She put books on her front porch

**第26题:** What is the mobile library?
**答案:** A library that visits different neighborhoods on Saturdays

**第27题:** What can we learn about Maria?
**答案:** She is passionate about children's education and community service

## C篇（说明文）

**话题:** 城市绿化与心理健康

**Passage:**

A growing body of research suggests that urban green spaces have significant benefits for mental health. A study published in the journal Environmental Health Perspectives tracked 10,000 city residents over five years and found that those living within 300 meters of a park or garden reported 20% lower levels of stress and anxiety compared to those without nearby green access.

The benefits extend beyond stress reduction. Researchers at the University of Exeter found that people who spent at least 120 minutes per week in nature were significantly more likely to report good health and high well-being. The effect was consistent across all age groups and income levels.

Scientists believe several mechanisms are at work. Green spaces encourage physical activity, provide opportunities for social interaction, and offer a restorative environment that helps the brain recover from mental fatigue. Trees and plants also improve air quality and reduce noise pollution — both of which are linked to better mental health outcomes.

Some cities are already taking action. Singapore's "City in a Garden" initiative has increased green cover from 36% to 47% over the past two decades. Copenhagen has committed to ensuring that all residents live within a 15-minute walk of a green space by 2030.

**第28题:** What did the study find?
**答案:** People near parks had 20% lower stress levels

**第29题:** How much time in nature per week is beneficial?
**答案:** At least 120 minutes

**第30题:** Which is NOT mentioned as a benefit of green spaces?
**答案:** Reducing housing costs（文中未提及）

**第31题:** What is the main purpose of the passage?
**答案:** To show the mental health benefits of urban green spaces

## D篇（议论文/科普文）

**话题:** 人工智能在医疗诊断中的应用与挑战

**Passage:**

Artificial intelligence is transforming medical diagnosis. AI systems can now detect certain cancers in medical images with accuracy that matches or exceeds that of experienced radiologists. In dermatology, AI tools have shown remarkable ability to identify skin cancer from photographs. In ophthalmology, AI can screen for diabetic retinopathy, a leading cause of blindness.

However, the adoption of AI in healthcare faces several challenges. First, AI systems are only as good as the data they are trained on. If training data lacks diversity, the system may perform poorly for certain populations. A dermatology AI trained mostly on light-skinned patients, for example, may miss cancers in darker-skinned individuals.

Second, there is the "black box" problem. Many AI systems cannot explain how they reached a particular diagnosis. This lack of transparency makes it difficult for doctors to trust and verify AI recommendations, and raises legal questions about accountability when errors occur.

Third, integrating AI into existing healthcare workflows requires significant investment in technology and training. Many hospitals, particularly in developing countries, lack the infrastructure to support AI systems.

Despite these challenges, the potential is enormous. The World Health Organization estimates that AI-assisted diagnosis could save 500,000 lives annually in developing countries by 2030.

**第32题:** What can AI do in medical diagnosis?
**答案:** Detect cancers with accuracy matching experienced doctors

**第33题:** What is the "black box" problem?
**答案:** AI cannot explain how it reached a diagnosis

**第34题:** Why might AI perform poorly for some populations?
**答案:** Training data lacks diversity

**第35题:** What is the author's attitude toward AI in healthcare?
**答案:** Cautiously optimistic（承认挑战但看好前景）

## 七选五 (36-40)

**话题:** 如何有效做笔记

**Passage:**

Taking good notes is an essential skill for students. Here are some tips to help you take better notes.

**36.** Before class, review the material from the previous lesson and preview the new topic. This will help you understand what the teacher is saying and identify the most important points.

**37.** Don't try to write down everything the teacher says. Focus on key concepts, definitions, and examples. Use abbreviations and symbols to save time.

**38.** Organize your notes using headings, bullet points, and numbered lists. This makes it easier to review later and find specific information.

**39.** After class, review your notes within 24 hours. Fill in any gaps, clarify unclear points, and add your own thoughts and connections.

**40.** Consider using different colors to highlight important information. For example, use red for key terms and blue for examples. This visual system helps your brain organize and remember the material.

A. Use a consistent system of abbreviations
B. Review and revise your notes regularly
C. Prepare before class
D. Focus on the main ideas, not every word
E. Use visual aids like colors and diagrams
F. Write neatly so others can read your notes
G. Use a clear structure

**答案:** 36-C, 37-D, 38-G, 39-B, 40-E
