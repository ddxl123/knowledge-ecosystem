# 2024高考英语全国一卷 · 听力

## Part 1 短对话 (1-5)

### 第1题
**原文:** W: Excuse me, could you tell me where the nearest bank is?
M: Go straight and turn left at the traffic lights. It's next to the post office.
**问题:** Where does the conversation take place?
**答案:** On the street
**解析:** 女士询问最近的银行在哪里，男士给出方向指引，说明对话发生在街上问路场景。

### 第2题
**原文:** M: The movie starts at 7:30. Shall we meet at 7:00?
W: I need to pick up my sister first. How about 7:15?
M: OK, see you then.
**问题:** When will they meet?
**答案:** At 7:15
**解析:** 男士提议7:00见面，女士因要接妹妹改为7:15，男士同意。

### 第3题
**原文:** W: I'd like to return this book. I borrowed it last week.
M: May I see your library card, please?
**问题:** Where are the speakers?
**答案:** At a library
**解析:** 女士要还书，男士要求出示借书证，场景为图书馆。

### 第4题
**原文:** M: How do you like your new apartment?
W: It's nice but a bit noisy. The traffic outside keeps me awake at night.
**问题:** What is the woman's problem?
**答案:** The noise from traffic
**解析:** 女士觉得公寓不错但外面的交通噪音让她晚上睡不着。

### 第5题
**原文:** W: Would you like some coffee or tea?
M: Neither, thanks. I'll just have some water.
**问题:** What will the man drink?
**答案:** Water
**解析:** 男士拒绝了咖啡和茶，只要水。

## Part 2 长对话 (6-9)

### Conversation 1
**话题:** 预约医生

W: Good morning, I'd like to make an appointment with Dr. Smith.
M: What's the matter?
W: I've had a headache for three days.
M: Is tomorrow morning at 10 OK?
W: Could I make it in the afternoon? I have a meeting in the morning.
M: How about 2:30?
W: That's perfect.

**第6题:** Why does the woman call?
**答案:** To make a doctor's appointment

**第7题:** When will the woman see the doctor?
**答案:** Tomorrow afternoon at 2:30

### Conversation 2
**话题:** 讨论周末计划

M: What are you planning to do this weekend?
W: I was thinking of going hiking. Would you like to join me?
M: I'd love to, but I promised to help my brother move to his new house.
W: That's OK. We can go next weekend instead.
M: Great! Let's check the weather forecast first.

**第8题:** What does the man have to do this weekend?
**答案:** Help his brother move

**第9题:** What will they do next weekend?
**答案:** Go hiking together

## Part 3 独白 (10-12)

**话题:** 社区志愿服务通知

Good morning, everyone. I'm calling to let you know about our community volunteer day this Saturday. We'll be cleaning up the park near the river from 9 AM to noon. Please wear comfortable clothes and bring gloves if you have them. We'll provide trash bags and drinking water. After the cleanup, we'll have a barbecue lunch for all volunteers. If you'd like to participate, please sign up at the community center by Friday.

**第10题:** When is the volunteer day?
**答案:** This Saturday

**第11题:** What will volunteers do?
**答案:** Clean up a park

**第12题:** What will happen after the cleanup?
**答案:** A barbecue lunch

## Part 4 听力填空 (13-15)

**话题:** 酒店预订

W: Good afternoon, Grand Hotel. How can I help you?
M: I'd like to book a room for three nights, from June 15th to 17th.
W: We have single rooms at $80 per night and double rooms at $120.
M: I'll take a single room. My name is Tom Brown, B-R-O-W-N.
W: Got it. Do you need a parking space?
M: Yes, please.

**第13题:** Price of single room: **$80 per night**
**第14题:** Guest's name: **Tom Brown**
**第15题:** Extra service: **Parking space**
