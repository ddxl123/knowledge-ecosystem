# 2024高考英语全国二卷 · 写作

## 第一节 短文改错

### 原文（含10处错误）

Dear Tom,

How are you? I'm writing to tell you about our school's annual sports meeting, that was held last Friday. It was a greatly success. Over 500 students took part in various event, including running, jumping and throwing.

I competed in the 800-meters race. Although I didn't win, but I finished the race and got the fourth place. My best friend Li Ming did much more better — he won first prize in the high jump! We were all very proudly of him.

The most exciting moment was when our class won the relay race. Everyone cheered and hugged each other. I was so happy that I could hardly fall asleep that night.

I hope you can come and watch it next year.

Yours,
Li Hua

### 改错答案

1. **that → which** （非限制性定语从句用which不用that）
2. **greatly → great** （修饰名词success用形容词）
3. **event → events** （various后接复数名词）
4. **800-meters → 800-meter** （复合形容词中名词不加s）
5. **去掉but** （although和but不能同时使用）
6. **去掉the** （序数词作状语时不加the：got fourth place）
7. **more → much** （better已经是比较级，much修饰比较级）
8. **proudly → proud** （be proud of 固定搭配）
9. **√** （此句无错误）
10. **it → them / the sports meeting** （指代需明确）

## 第二节 书面表达

### 题目
假定你是李华，你的学校将举办英语戏剧节（English Drama Festival）。请你给外教Mr. Brown写一封邮件，邀请他担任评委，内容包括：
1. 戏剧节的时间和地点；
2. 评委的工作内容；
3. 表达期待。

注意：1. 词数100左右；2. 可以适当增加细节。

### 参考范文

Dear Mr. Brown,

I'm Li Hua from Class 3. I'm writing to invite you to be a judge at our school's English Drama Festival.

The festival will be held in the school auditorium on December 20th, from 2 PM to 5 PM. Eight classes will perform short English plays they've been preparing for the past month.

As a judge, you would watch each performance and score them based on three criteria: language accuracy, acting skills, and creativity. After all performances, you would also give brief comments and present awards to the winners.

We would be truly honored to have you as our judge, as your expertise in English drama would be invaluable. Your feedback would mean a great deal to the students.

Please let me know if you're available. We're all looking forward to your participation!

Best regards,
Li Hua

### 写作要点分析

**结构:** 开头邀请→中间说明→结尾期待

**亮点句型:**
- I'm writing to invite you to...（开门见山）
- ...would be invaluable（表达价值）
- Your feedback would mean a great deal to...（强调意义）

**得分关键:**
- 要点完整：时间地点、工作内容、期待都覆盖
- 语言得体：邀请信的礼貌语气
- 逻辑连贯：使用连接词使行文流畅
