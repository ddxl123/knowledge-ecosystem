## A篇（应用文）

**话题:** 四个线上学习平台

**Passage:**

**Khan Academy** — Free online courses in math, science, and more. Video lessons with practice exercises. Suitable for all ages. Available in 40+ languages.

**Coursera** — University-level courses from top institutions like Stanford and Yale. Certificate programs available for a fee. Flexible schedule — learn at your own pace.

**Duolingo** — Learn a new language with fun, game-like lessons. 15 minutes a day is all you need. Free with optional premium subscription. 35+ languages available.

**Codecademy** — Learn to code with interactive, hands-on projects. Courses in Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and more. Free basic plan; Pro plan includes real-world projects.

**第21题:** Which platform is completely free?
**答案:** Khan Academy（明确标注Free）

**第22题:** What is special about Duolingo?
**答案:** It uses game-like lessons and only needs 15 minutes a day

**第23题:** Who would most likely use Codecademy?
**答案:** Someone interested in learning programming

## B篇（记叙文）

**话题:** 一位年轻发明家的故事

**Passage:**

At just 16 years old, Emma Chen has already patented two inventions. Her first, a solar-powered water purifier, was inspired by a trip to rural Guatemala where she saw children drinking contaminated water.

"I knew I had to do something," Emma recalls. She spent two years developing a portable device that uses solar energy to purify water. The device can clean up to 10 liters of water per hour and costs less than $20 to produce.

Her second invention came from a different experience. After her grandmother was diagnosed with diabetes, Emma created a non-invasive blood sugar monitor that uses light sensors instead of needles. The device is still being tested but has shown promising results.

Emma credits her success to her school's innovation program, which pairs students with professional mentors. "Without my mentor, Dr. James Liu, I would never have known where to start," she says.

Now a senior in high school, Emma plans to study biomedical engineering in college. She hopes to continue developing affordable medical devices for communities in need.

**第24题:** What inspired Emma's first invention?
**答案:** Seeing children drinking contaminated water in Guatemala

**第25题:** How much does the water purifier cost to produce?
**答案:** Less than $20

**第26题:** What is special about the blood sugar monitor?
**答案:** It uses light sensors instead of needles

**第27题:** What does Emma plan to study in college?
**答案:** Biomedical engineering

## C篇（说明文）

**话题:** 睡眠与记忆的关系

**Passage:**

Scientists have long known that sleep is important for memory, but recent research has revealed exactly how this process works. During sleep, the brain replays experiences from the day, strengthening the neural connections that form memories.

A study at the University of California found that participants who slept after learning a new task performed 20% better on a test the next day compared to those who stayed awake. The researchers used brain scans to show that during deep sleep, the hippocampus — the brain's memory center — "replays" the day's events at high speed.

The type of sleep also matters. Deep sleep, which occurs in the first half of the night, is crucial for factual memory — remembering names, dates, and events. REM sleep, more common in the second half of the night, is important for procedural memory — learning skills like playing a musical instrument or riding a bicycle.

This research has practical implications for students. "Pulling an all-nighter before an exam is actually counterproductive," says Dr. Sarah Mitchell, a sleep researcher. "You're much better off studying and then getting a full night's sleep."

**第28题:** What happens during sleep?
**答案:** The brain replays experiences to strengthen memories

**第29题:** How much better did sleepers perform?
**答案:** 20% better than those who stayed awake

**第30题:** What is deep sleep important for?
**答案:** Factual memory (names, dates, events)

**第31题:** What does Dr. Mitchell recommend?
**答案:** Study and then get a full night's sleep

## D篇（议论文）

**话题:** 远程工作对未来城市的影响

**Passage:**

The COVID-19 pandemic forced millions of workers to work from home, and many have never returned to the office full-time. This shift has profound implications for cities.

Office vacancy rates in major cities have reached record highs. In San Francisco, nearly 30% of office space sits empty. This has led to decreased property tax revenue, threatening funding for public services like schools and transportation.

However, some see opportunity in this change. Empty office buildings could be converted into affordable housing, addressing the chronic housing shortage in many cities. New York City has already begun offering tax incentives for such conversions.

Remote work has also changed where people choose to live. Without the need to commute, many workers have moved to smaller cities and rural areas, bringing new economic activity to these communities. Towns that were losing population are now growing again.

The challenge for city planners is to adapt to this new reality. "Cities that embrace the change and reinvent themselves will thrive," says urban planning expert Dr. Michael Torres. "Those that cling to the old model of everyone commuting to a central business district will struggle."

**第32题:** What has caused high office vacancy rates?
**答案:** The shift to remote work after COVID-19

**第33题:** What opportunity does the author mention?
**答案:** Converting offices into affordable housing

**第34题:** How has remote work affected small towns?
**答案:** They are growing as workers move there

**第35题:** What does Dr. Torres suggest?
**答案:** Cities should adapt and reinvent themselves

## 七选五 (36-40)

**话题:** 如何培养良好的阅读习惯

**Passage:**

Reading is one of the best ways to learn and grow. Here are some tips to help you build a lasting reading habit.

**36.** Set a specific goal, such as reading for 20 minutes before bed or finishing one book per month. Having a clear target keeps you motivated.

**37.** Find a comfortable, quiet spot where you can read without distractions. This could be a cozy corner of your room, a library, or a park bench.

**38.** Keep a book with you at all times. You never know when you'll have a few free minutes — waiting for a bus, during lunch break, or while waiting for an appointment.

**39.** Don't force yourself to finish a book you're not enjoying. Life is too short for boring books. Give it a fair chance (at least 50 pages), but if it doesn't grab you, move on.

**40.** Join a book club or share your reading list with friends. Discussing books with others makes reading more enjoyable and helps you discover new titles.

A. Always carry a book
B. Read what you enjoy
C. Set realistic goals
D. Create a reading-friendly environment
E. Share your reading experience
F. Read at the same time every day
G. Don't be afraid to quit a book

**答案:** 36-C, 37-D, 38-A, 39-G, 40-E
