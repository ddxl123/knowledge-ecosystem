# 高频词汇

高考英语中出现频率最高的核心词汇，按主题和频率整理。

## 高频动词

### achieve
- **音标**: /əˈtʃiːv/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 达到；取得；实现
- **高考例句**: He achieved great success through hard work.
- **搭配**: achieve one's goal/dream/success
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### acknowledge
- **音标**: /əkˈnɒlɪdʒ/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 承认；感谢；告知收到
- **高考例句**: She acknowledged that she had made a mistake.
- **搭配**: acknowledge doing sth; acknowledge...as/to be
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### acquire
- **音标**: /əˈkwaɪə(r)/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 获得；学到
- **高考例句**: She acquired a good knowledge of English.
- **搭配**: acquire knowledge/skills/habits
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### adapt
- **音标**: /əˈdæpt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 适应；改编
- **高考例句**: You should adapt yourself to the new environment.
- **搭配**: adapt to; adapt...from; adapt...for
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### advocate
- **音标**: /ˈædvəkeɪt/
- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 提倡；倡导者
- **高考例句**: He advocates protecting the environment.
- **搭配**: advocate doing sth
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### appreciate
- **音标**: /əˈpriːʃieɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 感激；欣赏；理解
- **高考例句**: I would appreciate it if you could help me.
- **搭配**: appreciate doing sth; I would appreciate it if...
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### claim
- **音标**: /kleɪm/
- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 声称；索取；索赔
- **高考例句**: He claims to have seen the accident.
- **搭配**: make a claim; claim to do/have done
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### commit
- **音标**: /kəˈmɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 承诺；犯（罪）；投入
- **高考例句**: He committed himself to finishing the project.
- **搭配**: commit a crime; commit oneself to; commit suicide
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### convey
- **音标**: /kənˈveɪ/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 传达；运送
- **高考例句**: Words cannot convey my feelings.
- **搭配**: convey a message/meaning/feelings
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### convince
- **音标**: /kənˈvɪns/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 使确信；说服
- **高考例句**: I'm convinced that he is honest.
- **搭配**: convince sb of sth; convince sb to do sth; be convinced that
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### decline
- **音标**: /dɪˈklaɪn/
- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 下降；衰退；谢绝
- **高考例句**: The number of students has declined sharply.
- **搭配**: in decline; on the decline; decline an invitation
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### deserve
- **音标**: /dɪˈzɜːv/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 值得；应得
- **高考例句**: He deserves to be praised for his courage.
- **搭配**: deserve to do; deserve doing = deserve to be done
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### distinguish

- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 区分；辨别；使出众

- **搭配**: distinguish...from...; distinguish oneself
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### guarantee

- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 保证；担保
- **高考例句**: Hard work does not guarantee success.
- **搭配**: guarantee to do; guarantee sth; give a guarantee
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### indicate

- **释义**: 表明；指出

- **搭配**: indicate that...; indicate sth to sb

### maintain
- **音标**: /meɪnˈteɪn/

- **释义**: 维持；坚持认为；保养
- **高考例句**: He maintains that he is innocent.
- **搭配**: maintain that...; maintain a balance; maintain health
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### obtain
- **音标**: /əbˈteɪn/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 获得；得到
- **高考例句**: She obtained a degree in English.

- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### promote
- **音标**: /prəˈməʊt/

- **搭配**: promote development; promote sb to; be promoted
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### purchase
- **音标**: /ˈpɜːtʃəs/
- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 购买；购置
- **高考例句**: The equipment was purchased at a reasonable price.
- **搭配**: make a purchase; purchase sth
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### recommend
- **音标**: /ˌrekəˈmend/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 推荐；建议
- **高考例句**: I recommend that you (should) read this book.
- **搭配**: recommend doing sth; recommend that... (should) do
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### reveal
- **音标**: /rɪˈviːl/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 揭示；透露
- **高考例句**: The survey reveals that most people support the plan.
- **搭配**: reveal that...; reveal a secret
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### struggle
- **音标**: /ˈstrʌɡl/
- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 奋斗；挣扎
- **高考例句**: She struggled to control her emotions.
- **搭配**: struggle with/against; struggle to do sth
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### substitute
- **音标**: /ˈsʌbstɪtjuːt/
- **词性**: v./n.
- **释义**: 替代；代替者
- **高考例句**: Nothing can substitute for hard work.
- **搭配**: substitute A for B; a substitute for
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### survive
- **音标**: /səˈvaɪv/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 幸存；存活
- **高考例句**: Only two passengers survived the accident.
- **搭配**: survive from; survive on; survive the earthquake
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### transform

- **搭配**: transform...into...; transform from...to...
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

## 高频名词

### access
- **音标**: /ˈækses/
- **词性**: n./v.
- **释义**: 通道；使用权；进入
- **高考例句**: Students have access to the library.
- **搭配**: have access to; gain access to; give access to
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### barrier
- **音标**: /ˈbæriə(r)/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 障碍；屏障
- **高考例句**: Language can be a barrier to communication.
- **搭配**: a barrier to; language barrier
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### campaign
- **音标**: /kæmˈpeɪn/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 运动；战役；活动
- **高考例句**: They launched a campaign to protect wildlife.
- **搭配**: launch a campaign; a campaign against/for
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### consequence

### contribution

### curiosity

### opportunity

### phenomenon

### privilege

### reputation

### solution

- **搭配**: a solution to; find/come up with a solution

### strategy

### volunteer

## 高频形容词/副词

### accessible
- **音标**: /əkˈsesəbl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 可进入的；易理解的
- **高考例句**: The building is accessible to wheelchair users.
- **搭配**: be accessible to
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### ambitious
- **音标**: /æmˈbɪʃəs/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 有雄心的；野心勃勃的
- **高考例句**: She is an ambitious young woman.
- **搭配**: be ambitious to do sth
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### considerable
- **音标**: /kənˈsɪdərəbl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 相当大的；重要的
- **高考例句**: A considerable number of students passed the exam.
- **搭配**: a considerable amount/number of
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### controversial
- **音标**: /ˌkɒntrəˈvɜːʃl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 有争议的
- **高考例句**: The topic is highly controversial.
- **搭配**: a controversial issue/topic/decision
- **高考频率**: ★★★

### essential
- **音标**: /ɪˈsenʃl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 必不可少的；本质的
- **高考例句**: Water is essential for life.
- **搭配**: be essential to/for; it is essential that... (should) do
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### gradually
- **音标**: /ˈɡrædʒuəli/
- **词性**: adv.
- **释义**: 逐渐地
- **高考例句**: The weather is gradually getting warmer.
- **搭配**: gradually improve/change/increase
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### reluctant
- **音标**: /rɪˈlʌktənt/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 不情愿的
- **高考例句**: He was reluctant to admit his mistake.
- **搭配**: be reluctant to do sth
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### significant
- **音标**: /sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 重要的；显著的
- **高考例句**: This is a significant change in policy.
- **搭配**: a significant improvement/increase/role
- **高考频率**: ★★★★★

### sustainable
- **音标**: /səˈsteɪnəbl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 可持续的
- **高考例句**: We need to develop sustainable energy sources.
- **搭配**: sustainable development; sustainable growth
- **高考频率**: ★★★★

### voluntarily
- **音标**: /ˌvɒlənˈterəli/
- **词性**: adv.
- **释义**: 自愿地
- **高考例句**: She voluntarily helped with the cleaning.
- **搭配**: voluntarily do sth
- **高考频率**: ★★★
