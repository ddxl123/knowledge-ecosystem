# 📘 按主题分类词汇

## 核心价值
按高考常考话题将3500词分类，便于在语境中记忆，提高词汇运用能力。

## 主题分类词汇示例

### 日常生活
- 饮食：ingredient, recipe, appetite, nutrition, organic
- 购物：discount, bargain, receipt, refund, guarantee
- 交通：commute, pedestrian, congestion, departure, transfer
- 住房：furniture, decoration, landlord, tenant, lease

### 学校教育
- 学科：curriculum, assignment, semester, scholarship, diploma
- 考试：review, revision, score, grade, certificate
- 校园：campus, dormitory, lecture, seminar, participate

### 科技发展
- 互联网：download, software, website, algorithm, digital
- 人工智能：intelligence, robot, automatic, data, analyze
- 新能源：solar, sustainable, renewable, carbon, emission

### 自然环境
- 气候：temperature, forecast, drought, flood, humidity
- 生态：ecosystem, species, habitat, conservation, pollution
- 灾害：earthquake, hurricane, disaster, rescue, shelter

## 记忆技巧
- 每个主题制作思维导图
- 用主题词汇造句或写小短文
- 同义词反义词对比记忆
- 结合阅读真题巩固主题词汇
