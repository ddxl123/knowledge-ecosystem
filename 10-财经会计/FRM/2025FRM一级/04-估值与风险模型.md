# Valuation and Risk Models

## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Value at Risk (VaR)
1. **Definition**: Maximum loss at a given confidence level over a given time horizon
2. **Methods**
   - Parametric (variance-covariance)
   - Historical simulation
   - Monte Carlo simulation
3. **VaR vs Expected Shortfall (ES)**
   - ES: average loss beyond VaR
   - ES is coherent; VaR is not

### 二、Market Risk
1. **Bond VaR**
   - Duration-based approximation
2. **Portfolio VaR**
   - Aggregation and diversification
3. **Backtesting VaR**
   - Kupiec test, traffic light approach

### 三、Credit Risk Models
1. **Credit scoring models**
2. **Structural models** (Merton model)
   - Firm value = debt + equity
   - Default when firm value < debt at maturity
3. **Reduced-form models**
4. **CreditMetrics**
5. **Credit Risk+**

### 四、Operational Risk
1. **Loss distribution approach**
2. **Scenario analysis**
3. **Key risk indicators**

### 五、Stress Testing
1. **Historical scenarios**
2. **Hypothetical scenarios**
3. **Reverse stress testing**

## Common Mistakes
1. ⚠️ VaR is not subadditive (not coherent); ES is
2. ⚠️ Parametric VaR assumes normal distribution
3. ⚠️ Merton model treats equity as a call option on firm assets
