## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Derivative Markets
1. **Forward commitments**: forwards, futures, swaps
2. **Contingent claims**: options

### 二、Forward Contracts
1. **Pricing**: F₀ = S₀ × (1+r)^T
2. **Valuation**: V = S - PV(F)
3. **Credit risk in forwards**

### 三、Futures Contracts
1. **Standardization**
2. **Margin requirements**
3. **Marking to market**
4. **Forwards vs futures**

### 四、Options
1. **Call and put options**
2. **Option payoff diagrams**
3. **Put-call parity**: C + PV(X) = P + S
4. **Option pricing factors**
   - Underlying price, strike, time, volatility, interest rate, dividends

### 五、Swaps
1. **Interest rate swaps**
2. **Currency swaps**
3. **Credit default swaps**
