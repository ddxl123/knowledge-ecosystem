## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Portfolio Risk and Return
1. **Expected return of portfolio**
2. **Portfolio variance and standard deviation**
3. **Diversification benefits**
   - Correlation < 1 → risk reduction

### 二、Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)
1. **Security Market Line (SML)**
2. **Beta**: systematic risk measure
3. **CAPM assumptions and limitations**

### 三、Portfolio Construction
1. **Asset allocation**
   - Strategic vs tactical
2. **Markowitz efficient frontier**
3. **Capital Market Line (CML)**

### 四、Investor Profiling
1. **Risk tolerance**
2. **Time horizon**
3. **Liquidity needs**
4. **Return objectives**

### 五、Active vs Passive Management
1. **Index funds**
2. **Active management**: alpha generation
3. **Semi-strong form efficiency implications**

### 六、Portfolio Performance Evaluation
1. **Sharpe ratio**: (Rₚ - Rf) / σₚ
2. **Treynor ratio**: (Rₚ - Rf) / βₚ
3. **Jensen's alpha**: Rₚ - [Rf + βₚ(Rm - Rf)]
4. **Information ratio**: α / σ(ε)

## Common Mistakes
1. ⚠️ Sharpe ratio uses total risk; Treynor ratio uses systematic risk
2. ⚠️ Diversification eliminates unsystematic risk only
3. ⚠️ CML applies to efficient portfolios; SML applies to all assets
