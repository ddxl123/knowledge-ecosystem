## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Time Value of Money
1. **Present Value & Future Value**
   - FV = PV × (1+r)^n
   - PV = FV / (1+r)^n
2. **Annuities**
   - Ordinary annuity vs annuity due
   - Perpetuity: PV = PMT / r
3. **Effective Annual Rate**
   - EAR = (1 + r/m)^m - 1

### 二、Statistical Concepts
1. **Descriptive Statistics**
   - Mean, median, mode
   - Variance, standard deviation
   - Skewness, kurtosis
2. **Probability Concepts**
   - Multiplication rule, addition rule
   - Conditional probability, Bayes' formula
3. **Probability Distributions**
   - Binomial distribution
   - Normal distribution
   - Lognormal distribution

### 三、Sampling and Estimation
1. **Sampling Methods**
2. **Central Limit Theorem**
3. **Confidence Intervals**
4. **Hypothesis Testing**
   - Type I and Type II errors
   - p-value
   - t-test, z-test, chi-square test, F-test

### 四、Linear Regression
1. **Simple Linear Regression**
   - Y = b₀ + b₁X + ε
   - R², standard error of estimate
2. **Assumptions**
3. **Hypothesis testing of coefficients**

## Common Mistakes
1. ⚠️ Annuity due vs ordinary annuity: payment timing matters
2. ⚠️ Type I error = rejecting true null (false positive)
3. ⚠️ R² measures goodness of fit, not causation
