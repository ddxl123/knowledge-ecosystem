# Economics

## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Microeconomics
1. **Supply and Demand**
   - Elasticity: price, income, cross-price
   - Consumer surplus, producer surplus
2. **Market Structures**
   - Perfect competition
   - Monopoly, monopolistic competition, oligopoly
3. **Production and Costs**
   - Marginal product, average product
   - Economies of scale

### 二、Macroeconomics
1. **GDP**
   - Expenditure approach: C + I + G + (X-M)
   - Income approach
2. **Business Cycles**
   - Expansion, peak, contraction, trough
3. **Inflation**
   - CPI, PPI
   - Demand-pull vs cost-push
4. **Monetary and Fiscal Policy**
   - Money supply, interest rates
   - Government spending, taxation
5. **International Trade**
   - Comparative advantage, absolute advantage
   - Balance of payments

### 三、Monetary and Fiscal Policy
1. **Central Bank Tools**
   - Open market operations, reserve requirements, discount rate
2. **Fiscal Policy**
   - Expansionary vs contractionary
   - Automatic stabilizers

## Exam Tips
- **High-frequency topics**: GDP calculation, elasticity, monetary policy
- **Weight**: 6-9% of exam

## Common Mistakes
1. ⚠️ GDP includes only final goods, not intermediate
2. ⚠️ Demand-pull inflation: too much money chasing too few goods
3. ⚠️ Comparative advantage vs absolute advantage
