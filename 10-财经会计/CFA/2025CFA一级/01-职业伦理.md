# Ethical and Professional Standards

## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Code of Ethics
1. Act with integrity, competence, diligence, respect, and in an ethical manner
2. Place clients' interests above own
3. Use reasonable care and independent professional judgment
4. Practice in a professional and ethical manner
5. Promote the integrity of and uphold the rules governing capital markets
6. Maintain and improve professional competence

### 二、Standards of Professional Conduct
1. **Professionalism**
   - Knowledge of the law
   - Independence and objectivity
   - Misrepresentation
   - Misconduct
2. **Integrity of Capital Markets**
   - Material nonpublic information (MNPI)
   - Market manipulation
3. **Duties to Clients**
   - Loyalty, prudence, and care
   - Fair dealing
   - Suitability
   - Performance presentation
   - Preservation of confidentiality
4. **Duties to Employers**
   - Loyalty
   - Additional compensation arrangements
   - Responsibilities of supervisors
5. **Investment Analysis, Recommendations, and Actions**
   - Diligence and reasonable basis
   - Communication with clients
   - Record retention
6. **Conflicts of Interest**
   - Disclosure of conflicts
   - Priority of transactions
   - Referral fees
7. **Responsibilities as a CFA Member**
   - Conduct as members and candidates
   - Reference to CFA Institute and designation

### 三、GIPS (Global Investment Performance Standards)
1. Composite construction
2. Calculation methodology
3. Presentation and reporting

## 2025 Updates

## Exam Tips
- **High-frequency topics**: Independence and objectivity, MNPI, fair dealing
- **Weight**: 15-20% of exam

## Common Mistakes
1. ⚠️ Mosaic theory: Using MNPI + public information = not a violation
2. ⚠️ Soft dollars must be used for the benefit of clients
3. ⚠️ Standards apply to all CFA members and candidates globally
