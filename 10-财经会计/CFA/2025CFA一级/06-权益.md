# Equity Investments

## Core Knowledge Points

### 一、Market Organization and Structure
1. **Market types**: exchange, OTC
2. **Order types**: market, limit, stop
3. **Market efficiency**: weak, semi-strong, strong

### 二、Security Market Indices
1. **Price-weighted, market-cap-weighted, equal-weighted**
2. **Rebalancing and reconstitution**

### 三、Equity Valuation
1. **DDM (Dividend Discount Model)**
   - Gordon Growth Model: P₀ = D₁ / (r - g)
   - Two-stage DDM
2. **Free Cash Flow Models**
   - FCFF, FCFE
3. **Market-based Valuation**
   - P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA

### 四、Industry and Company Analysis
1. **Industry life cycle**
2. **Porter's Five Forces**
3. **Competitive advantages**

## Exam Tips
- **High-frequency topics**: DDM, P/E ratio, market efficiency
- **Weight**: 10-12% of exam

## Common Mistakes
1. ⚠️ Gordon Growth Model assumes constant growth forever
2. ⚠️ Trailing P/E vs leading P/E
3. ⚠️ Market-cap-weighted indices have momentum bias
