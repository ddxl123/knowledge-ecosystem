# 告示类

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
2024王江涛高分写作小作文告示类的写作指导，包含格式规范、万能句型和范文。

### 知识结构
- **格式规范**：告示的标准格式
- **万能句型**：标题、正文、落款的常用表达
- **范文**：高分范文及赏析

### 待收集原子知识点
- 告示格式：告示类作文的标准格式规范
- 万能句型：标题拟定、正文表达的常用句型
- 范文分析：高分范文的结构和语言特点
## 写作框架
标题：NOTICE / ANNOUNCEMENT
第一段：活动/事件背景 + 发布目的
第二段：具体信息（时间、地点、内容、参与方式）
第三段：联系方式 + 期待参与

## 万能句型
### 标题
NOTICE / ANNOUNCEMENT

### 开头句
1. In order to ..., we are pleased to announce that...
2. A ... will be held on [date] at [venue].
3. We are delighted to inform you that...

### 中间句
1. The event will include...
2. Those who are interested are welcome to...
3. Please register before [deadline] by...

### 结尾句
1. For further information, please contact...
2. Your participation is highly welcome.
3. We look forward to your active involvement.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write a notice to recruit volunteers for a community service event.
**范文**:

NOTICE

In order to promote community engagement and foster a spirit of volunteerism, the Student Union is pleased to announce a community service event — "Green Neighborhood" — scheduled for Saturday, October 15th.

Volunteers will participate in activities including neighborhood cleanup, tree planting, and environmental awareness education. Those who are interested are welcome to sign up at the Student Union office (Room 201, Administration Building) before October 10th. Each volunteer will receive a certificate of participation and a commemorative T-shirt.

For further information, please contact Li Ming at 138-0000-0000 or email volunteer@university.edu.cn. Your participation is highly welcome!

Student Union
October 1, 2022

**亮点词汇/句型**: `promote community engagement`, `foster a spirit of volunteerism`, `commemorative`, `active involvement`
