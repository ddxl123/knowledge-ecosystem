# 推荐信

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
2024王江涛高分写作小作文书信类中推荐信的写作指导，包含格式规范、万能句型和范文。

### 知识结构
- **格式规范**：书信的标准格式
- **万能句型**：开头、正文、结尾的常用表达
- **范文**：高分范文及赏析

### 待收集原子知识点
- 书信格式：推荐信的标准格式规范
- 万能句型：开头问候、正文表达、结尾祝愿的常用句型
- 范文分析：高分范文的结构和语言特点
## 写作框架
第一段：说明推荐目的 + 与被推荐人关系
第二段：被推荐人的能力/品质/成就（2-3点）
第三段：总结推荐 + 提供联系方式

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to recommend ... for...
2. It is my great pleasure to recommend ... to you.
3. I would like to recommend ... without reservation.

### 中间句
1. During the time I worked with him/her, I was deeply impressed by...
2. He/She possesses several outstanding qualities, including...
3. What distinguishes him/her from others is...

### 结尾句
1. I highly recommend ... and believe he/she will be an excellent candidate.
2. Should you require any further information, please feel free to contact me.
3. I am confident that ... will exceed your expectations.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to recommend a book to a foreign friend.
**范文**:
Dear Tom,

I am writing to recommend a book that I believe you would find both enlightening and enjoyable — "Erta" by Liu Cixin, a renowned Chinese science fiction author.

What distinguishes this book from others is its unique blend of Eastern philosophy and Western scientific concepts. The story explores the fascinating topic of the three-body problem in astrophysics, while weaving in profound questions about humanity's place in the universe. During the time I spent reading it, I was deeply impressed by the author's ability to make complex scientific theories accessible and thrilling.

I highly recommend this book and believe it will provide you with a fresh perspective on Chinese literature. Should you require any further recommendations, please feel free to contact me.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `enlightening and enjoyable`, `renowned`, `unique blend of`, `accessible and thrilling`
