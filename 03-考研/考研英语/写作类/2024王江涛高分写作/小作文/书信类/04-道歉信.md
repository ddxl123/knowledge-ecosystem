# 道歉信

## 写作框架
第一段：表达歉意 + 说明道歉原因
第二段：解释原因 + 提出补救措施
第三段：再次道歉 + 请求原谅

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my sincere apology for...
2. I am terribly sorry that I failed to...
3. Please accept my heartfelt apology for...

### 中间句
1. The reason for my absence/mistake was that...
2. To make up for it, I would like to...
3. I assure you that this will not happen again.

### 结尾句
1. Once again, I apologize for any inconvenience caused.
2. I sincerely hope you can accept my apology.
3. Please give me a chance to make it up to you.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2021年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to apologize for being unable to attend a friend's wedding.
**范文**:
Dear David,

I am writing to express my sincere apology for being unable to attend your wedding ceremony next Saturday. I was truly looking forward to celebrating this special occasion with you and Mary.

Unfortunately, I have just been informed that I must attend an important academic conference in Beijing on the same date. As a key presenter, my absence would cause significant inconvenience to the organizers. I understand how important this day is for you, and I feel terrible about missing it.

To make up for it, I would like to invite you and Mary to dinner at your convenience after your honeymoon. Please accept my heartfelt congratulations and this small gift as a token of my best wishes for your future together.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `celebrating this special occasion`, `cause significant inconvenience`, `at your convenience`, `a token of`
