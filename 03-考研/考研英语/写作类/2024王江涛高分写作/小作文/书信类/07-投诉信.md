# 投诉信

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
2024王江涛高分写作小作文书信类中投诉信的写作指导，包含格式规范、万能句型和范文。

### 知识结构
- **格式规范**：书信的标准格式
- **万能句型**：开头、正文、结尾的常用表达
- **范文**：高分范文及赏析

### 待收集原子知识点
- 书信格式：投诉信的标准格式规范
- 万能句型：开头问候、正文表达、结尾祝愿的常用句型
- 范文分析：高分范文的结构和语言特点
## 写作框架
第一段：自我介绍 + 投诉内容
第二段：具体问题描述 + 已采取的措施
第三段：提出解决方案 + 期望回复

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with...
2. I am writing to complain about...
3. I regret to inform you that I am not satisfied with...

### 中间句
1. The problem is that... / What troubles me most is...
2. To my great disappointment, ...
3. I have tried to resolve this issue by..., but in vain.

### 结尾句
1. I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter and take immediate action.
2. I trust you will give this matter your prompt attention.
3. I hope this problem can be resolved as soon as possible.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to complain about a defective product purchased online.
**范文**:
Dear Customer Service Manager,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with a laptop I recently purchased from your online store (Order No. JD20230515). To my great disappointment, the product has several serious defects.

The most troubling issue is that the battery drains completely within two hours, far below the advertised eight-hour battery life. Additionally, the screen flickers intermittently, making it nearly impossible to work for extended periods. I have tried to resolve this issue by following the troubleshooting guide on your website, but the problems persist.

I would appreciate it if you could arrange for a replacement or a full refund at your earliest convenience. I trust you will give this matter your prompt attention and look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `express my dissatisfaction`, `to my great disappointment`, `intermittently`, `at your earliest convenience`
