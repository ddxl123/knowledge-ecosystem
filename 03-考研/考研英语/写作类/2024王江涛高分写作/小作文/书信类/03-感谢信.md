## 写作框架
第一段：表达感谢 + 说明感谢原因
第二段：具体描述对方的帮助及其意义
第三段：再次感谢 + 表达回报意愿

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for...
2. I would like to take this opportunity to thank you for...
3. Words fail to convey my heartfelt appreciation for...

### 中间句
1. Your generous help has made a significant difference in...
2. Without your support, I would not have been able to...
3. It was your encouragement that gave me the confidence to...

### 结尾句
1. I hope I can repay your kindness in the near future.
2. Please accept my deepest thanks once again.
3. Should you need any help in the future, please do not hesitate to let me know.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to express gratitude to someone who helped you during a difficult time.
**范文**:
Dear Professor Johnson,

I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for the invaluable guidance you provided during my thesis writing process. Your patience and expertise have been a tremendous source of support for me.

Without your insightful suggestions, I would not have been able to overcome the difficulties I encountered in data analysis. It was your constant encouragement that gave me the confidence to persevere when I felt overwhelmed. Moreover, the reference materials you recommended proved extremely helpful in shaping my research framework.

Please accept my deepest thanks once again. I hope I will have the opportunity to repay your kindness in the future. Should you need any assistance, please do not hesitate to let me know.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `invaluable guidance`, `tremendous source of support`, `insightful suggestions`, `persevere`
