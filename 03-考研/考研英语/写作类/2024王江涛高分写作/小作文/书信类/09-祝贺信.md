## 写作框架
第一段：表达祝贺 + 说明祝贺原因
第二段：对对方成就的肯定 + 祝福
第三段：再次祝贺 + 期望未来

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my warmest congratulations on...
2. It is with great pleasure that I congratulate you on...
3. I was thrilled to hear that you have...

### 中间句
1. Your achievement is a well-deserved recognition of...
2. This success is the result of your hard work and dedication.
3. No one deserves this honor more than you.

### 结尾句
1. Please accept my heartfelt congratulations once again.
2. I wish you continued success in all your future endeavors.
3. May this be just the beginning of a bright and prosperous future.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to congratulate a friend on winning a scholarship.
**范文**:
Dear Wang Ming,

I am writing to express my warmest congratulations on your winning the National Scholarship. I was thrilled to hear this wonderful news, and no one deserves this honor more than you.

This achievement is a well-deserved recognition of your outstanding academic performance and tireless efforts over the past three years. I still remember how you stayed up late in the library preparing for exams and conducting research. Your dedication and perseverance have truly paid off, setting an excellent example for all of us.

Please accept my heartfelt congratulations once again. I wish you continued success in your future studies and career. May this be just the beginning of a bright and prosperous future.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `warmest congratulations`, `well-deserved recognition`, `tireless efforts`, `dedication and perseverance`
