# 文化交流类（图画作文）

## 写作框架
第一段：描述图画 + 文化交流主题
第二段：文化交流的意义/挑战
第三段：建议/展望

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is vividly depicted in the picture, people from different cultural backgrounds are...
2. The picture presents a scene of cultural exchange in which...
3. This drawing highlights the significance of cross-cultural communication.

### 中间句
1. Cultural exchange serves as a bridge connecting different nations.
2. On the one hand, it promotes mutual understanding and respect.
3. On the other hand, we should preserve our own cultural identity while embracing diversity.

### 结尾句
1. In conclusion, cultural exchange is essential for building a harmonious world.
2. We should embrace cultural diversity while cherishing our own heritage.
3. Only through open-minded dialogue can we achieve true cultural prosperity.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing people from different countries celebrating a festival together.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, people from various countries are celebrating the Spring Festival together, wearing traditional Chinese costumes and sharing dumplings. This heartwarming scene highlights the growing influence of Chinese culture on the global stage.

Cultural exchange serves as a bridge connecting different nations and peoples. On the one hand, it promotes mutual understanding and respect, breaking down stereotypes and prejudices. When foreigners experience Chinese festivals firsthand, they gain a deeper appreciation of Chinese values and traditions. On the other hand, we should preserve our own cultural identity while embracing diversity. Cultural exchange is not about assimilation but about mutual enrichment.

In conclusion, cultural exchange is essential for building a harmonious world. We should be proud ambassadors of our own culture while showing genuine interest in learning from others. Only through open-minded dialogue can we achieve true cultural prosperity and global peace.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `heartwarming scene`, `growing influence`, `breaking down stereotypes`, `mutual enrichment`
