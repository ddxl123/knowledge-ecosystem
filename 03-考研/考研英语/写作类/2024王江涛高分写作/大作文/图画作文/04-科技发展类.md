# 科技发展类（图画作文）

## 写作框架
第一段：描述图画 + 科技主题
第二段：科技的利与弊分析
第三段：理性看待 + 建议

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is vividly depicted in the picture, ...
2. The picture raises a thought-provoking question about the role of technology in modern life.
3. This drawing reflects the profound impact of technological advancement on...

### 中间句
1. On the positive side, technology has revolutionized the way we...
2. However, we cannot ignore the potential drawbacks of...
3. The key lies in how we harness technology rather than being enslaved by it.

### 结尾句
1. In conclusion, technology is a double-edged sword.
2. We should embrace technological innovation while remaining vigilant about its side effects.
3. Only by using technology wisely can we truly benefit from its enormous potential.

## 真题范文（2021-2024）
### 2024年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing the contrast between online and offline communication.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, two scenes are presented side by side: in one, a group of friends are chatting and laughing together at a café; in the other, the same people are sitting together but staring at their smartphones in silence. This drawing reflects the profound impact of technology on interpersonal communication.

On the positive side, technology has revolutionized the way we connect with others, making it possible to communicate across vast distances instantly. However, we cannot ignore the potential drawbacks. The over-reliance on digital communication has led to a decline in face-to-face interactions, weakening the quality of human relationships. The key lies in how we harness technology rather than being enslaved by it.

In conclusion, technology is a double-edged sword. While we should embrace the convenience it offers, we must not allow it to replace genuine human connection. Only by using technology wisely can we truly benefit from its enormous potential while preserving the warmth of real-life interactions.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `side by side`, `revolutionized the way`, `over-reliance on`, `a double-edged sword`
