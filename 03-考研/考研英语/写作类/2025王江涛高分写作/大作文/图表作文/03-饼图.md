## 写作框架
第一段：描述饼图数据 + 各部分占比
第二段：分析原因/特征
第三段：总结 + 建议/预测

## 万能句型
### 开头句（描述数据）
1. As is clearly shown in the pie chart, ... accounts for the largest proportion at...%, followed by... at...%.
2. The pie chart illustrates the distribution of... among...
3. According to the pie chart, the most striking feature is that... constitutes approximately...% of the total.

### 中间句（分析原因）
1. The dominance of... can be attributed to...
2. Several factors contribute to this distribution pattern.
3. The data reveals a clear preference for... over...

### 结尾句（总结建议）
1. In conclusion, the pie chart highlights the need to...
2. Based on the data, it is evident that... deserves more attention.
3. To achieve a more balanced distribution, efforts should be made to...

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a pie chart showing how college students spend their daily free time.
**范文**:
As is clearly shown in the pie chart, the ways in which college students spend their daily free time vary significantly. Social media and short videos account for the largest proportion at 35%, followed by online gaming at 20% and sports at 15%. Reading occupies 12% of free time, while social activities and other pursuits make up the remaining 18%.

Several factors contribute to this distribution pattern. The dominance of social media and short videos can be attributed to the addictive nature of these platforms and the instant gratification they provide. The popularity of online gaming reflects the appeal of virtual entertainment among young people. In contrast, the relatively low proportion of time devoted to reading may indicate a shift in information consumption habits, as students increasingly prefer visual and audio content over traditional text-based materials. However, it is worth noting that a certain amount of recreational time is essential for mental health and stress relief.

In conclusion, while entertainment is a necessary part of student life, the data highlights the need for a more balanced allocation of free time. Students should consciously allocate more time to reading, physical exercise, and face-to-face social interactions. Universities can also play a role by organizing diverse extracurricular activities that promote intellectual and physical development.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `vary significantly`, `instant gratification`, `addictive nature`, `information consumption habits`, `balanced allocation`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a pie chart showing the sources of air pollution in a major city.
**范文**:
As is clearly shown in the pie chart, the sources of air pollution in the city are diverse. Vehicle emissions constitute the largest proportion at 40%, followed by industrial emissions at 25% and construction dust at 15%. Household energy consumption accounts for 12%, while other sources make up the remaining 8%.

The dominance of vehicle emissions can be attributed to the rapid increase in private car ownership and inadequate public transportation infrastructure. Industrial emissions remain a significant contributor due to the concentration of factories in urban and suburban areas. The relatively high proportion of construction dust reflects the ongoing urbanization and infrastructure development. This distribution reveals that air pollution is a multifaceted problem requiring comprehensive solutions.

To address this issue effectively, a multi-pronged approach is necessary. The government should invest in public transportation, enforce stricter emission standards for vehicles and factories, and promote the use of clean energy. Citizens should also be encouraged to adopt green commuting habits such as cycling and using public transit. Only through coordinated efforts at all levels can we achieve cleaner air and a healthier living environment.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `diverse`, `inadequate public transportation`, `multifaceted problem`, `multi-pronged approach`, `coordinated efforts`
