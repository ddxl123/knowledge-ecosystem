## 写作框架
第一段：描述图表数据 + 总体趋势
第二段：分析原因/变化
第三段：总结 + 预测/建议

## 万能句型
### 开头句（描述数据）
1. As is clearly shown in the bar chart, ... experienced a significant increase/decrease from... to...
2. According to the bar chart, the number/proportion of... rose/fell dramatically over the period of...
3. The bar chart illustrates the changes in... between... and...

### 中间句（分析变化）
1. Several factors may account for this trend. To begin with, ...
2. The reasons for this dramatic change are multifaceted.
3. This upward/downward trend can be attributed to...

### 结尾句（总结预测）
1. In conclusion, the data suggests that... will continue to... in the foreseeable future.
2. Based on the analysis above, it is reasonable to predict that...
3. To sustain this positive trend, it is essential that...

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a bar chart showing the changes in the number of Chinese tourists traveling abroad from 2018 to 2024.
**范文**:
As is clearly shown in the bar chart, the number of Chinese tourists traveling abroad experienced a remarkable transformation between 2018 and 2024. The figure rose steadily from 150 million in 2018 to 170 million in 2019, before plummeting to nearly 20 million in 2020 due to the pandemic. Subsequently, the number gradually recovered, reaching 130 million by 2024.

Several factors can account for this trend. The pre-pandemic growth was driven by rising disposable incomes, relaxed visa policies, and an increasing desire for international experiences among Chinese citizens. The dramatic decline in 2020 was primarily caused by global travel restrictions and health concerns. The subsequent recovery can be attributed to the lifting of travel bans, pent-up demand, and the government's support for the tourism industry. Furthermore, the rise of short-haul travel to neighboring countries has contributed to the rebound.

In conclusion, while the tourism industry has shown remarkable resilience, it is essential to continue investing in infrastructure and services to meet the growing demand. Based on the current trajectory, it is reasonable to predict that outbound tourism from China will surpass pre-pandemic levels within the next few years.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `remarkable transformation`, `plummeting to`, `pent-up demand`, `short-haul travel`, `remarkable resilience`, `current trajectory`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a bar chart comparing the employment rates of college graduates in different majors.
**范文**:
As is clearly shown in the bar chart, there are significant differences in the employment rates of college graduates across various majors. Computer Science leads with an employment rate of 95%, followed by Engineering at 88% and Business Administration at 82%. In contrast, the rates for Humanities and Fine Arts are notably lower, at 65% and 58% respectively.

Several factors may account for this disparity. First and foremost, the rapid development of the technology sector has created a surging demand for graduates in STEM fields. Additionally, practical skills and technical knowledge are highly valued in the current job market, giving an advantage to students in applied disciplines. However, it would be misleading to equate employment rate with the value of a major. Humanities and arts graduates often develop critical thinking, creativity, and communication skills that are essential for long-term career success, even if their initial employment rates are lower.

In conclusion, while the data highlights the importance of aligning education with market demands, we should not overlook the intrinsic value of all academic disciplines. To address the employment gap, universities should strengthen career guidance and internship programs for students in all fields.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `significant differences`, `surging demand`, `STEM fields`, `equated with`, `intrinsic value`
