## 写作框架
第一段：描述表格数据 + 关键对比
第二段：分析数据差异的原因
第三段：总结 + 建议/预测

## 万能句型
### 开头句（描述数据）
1. As is clearly presented in the table, there are significant differences in... between...
2. The table provides a detailed comparison of... across different categories.
3. According to the data in the table, ... shows the most dramatic variation among all categories.

### 中间句（分析对比）
1. The data reveals a stark contrast between... and...
2. Several factors may explain these differences.
3. The disparity between... and ... can be attributed to...

### 结尾句（总结建议）
1. In conclusion, the table highlights the need for targeted policies to address...
2. The data suggests that more attention should be paid to...
3. To bridge the gap, it is essential to...

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a table comparing the average daily screen time of different age groups.
**范文**:
As is clearly presented in the table, there are notable differences in average daily screen time across age groups. Teenagers (13-18) lead with 7.5 hours per day, followed by young adults (19-30) at 6.8 hours and middle-aged adults (31-50) at 5.2 hours. Seniors (51+) spend the least time on screens, averaging 3.1 hours daily.

Several factors may explain these differences. The high screen time among teenagers can be attributed to the integration of digital devices into education and the popularity of social media and online gaming. Young adults, being digital natives, rely heavily on screens for both work and entertainment. The lower screen time among seniors reflects their different lifestyle habits and relatively lower adoption of digital technology. However, it is concerning that all age groups exceed the recommended maximum of two hours of recreational screen time per day.

In conclusion, the table highlights the pervasive influence of digital devices in modern society. To mitigate the health risks associated with excessive screen time, individuals should practice the 20-20-20 rule (every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds) and set daily limits on recreational screen use. Schools and parents should also play an active role in promoting healthy digital habits among young people.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `notable differences`, `digital natives`, `pervasive influence`, `mitigate the health risks`, `healthy digital habits`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a table showing the top five reasons why employees leave their jobs.
**范文**:
As is clearly presented in the table, the top five reasons for employee turnover are: low salary (32%), limited career advancement (25%), poor work-life balance (18%), unsatisfactory management (15%), and lack of job satisfaction (10%).

The data reveals a stark contrast between monetary and non-monetary factors. While low salary remains the leading cause, the combined proportion of non-monetary factors (68%) far exceeds it. This suggests that employees increasingly value personal growth, well-being, and a positive work environment over financial compensation alone. The high ranking of limited career advancement indicates that many organizations fail to provide clear development paths for their employees, leading to frustration and eventual departure.

To reduce turnover, organizations should adopt a holistic approach to employee retention. Competitive compensation is important, but equally crucial are opportunities for professional development, flexible working arrangements, and effective leadership. By addressing the diverse needs of their workforce, organizations can create an environment where employees feel valued, motivated, and committed to long-term growth.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `employee turnover`, `stark contrast`, `holistic approach`, `employee retention`, `flexible working arrangements`
