# 文化交流类（图画作文）

## 写作框架
第一段：描述图画 + 揭示文化交流主题
第二段：分析文化交流的意义/挑战
第三段：总结 + 建议如何促进文化交流

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is vividly depicted in the picture, people from different cultural backgrounds are...
2. The drawing illustrates the importance of cultural exchange in the era of globalization.
3. This thought-provoking picture highlights the significance of...

### 中间句
1. Cultural exchange serves as a bridge that connects people from different backgrounds.
2. On the one hand, cultural exchange promotes mutual understanding and respect.
3. While cultural differences may lead to misunderstandings, they also provide opportunities for learning.

### 结尾句
1. In conclusion, we should embrace cultural diversity and promote cross-cultural communication.
2. Only by respecting and learning from each other's cultures can we build a harmonious global community.
3. It is our shared responsibility to preserve cultural heritage while embracing global integration.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2020年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a Chinese person and a foreigner exchanging gifts, symbolizing cultural exchange.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a Chinese student and a foreign friend are exchanging gifts with warm smiles, representing the spirit of cultural exchange. In the background, elements of both Eastern and Western cultures are intertwined, creating a harmonious scene.

Cultural exchange serves as a bridge that connects people from different backgrounds. In today's interconnected world, understanding and appreciating diverse cultures has become more important than ever. On the one hand, cultural exchange promotes mutual understanding, reduces prejudice, and fosters international cooperation. On the other hand, it allows individuals to broaden their horizons and develop a more nuanced worldview. Take the Confucius Institutes around the world as an example — they have successfully introduced Chinese language and culture to millions of people, while also facilitating Chinese students' understanding of other cultures.

In conclusion, we should embrace cultural diversity and actively promote cross-cultural communication. By learning from each other's traditions, values, and perspectives, we can build a more tolerant and harmonious global community.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `intertwined`, `fosters international cooperation`, `broaden their horizons`, `nuanced worldview`

### 2025年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing people from different countries collaborating on a project.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a group of people from different countries are working together on a collaborative project, with flags of various nations displayed in the background. This image highlights the growing importance of international cooperation in the modern world.

The significance of cross-cultural collaboration cannot be overstated. In an era of globalization, the challenges we face — from climate change to public health crises — transcend national boundaries and require collective action. When people from diverse backgrounds collaborate, they bring unique perspectives and skills that enrich the problem-solving process. Furthermore, such collaboration fosters mutual respect and understanding, laying the foundation for lasting peace and prosperity.

In my view, we should actively seek opportunities to collaborate with people from different cultures, both in academic and professional settings. Only by working together can we address the complex challenges of our time and create a better future for all.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `transcend national boundaries`, `collective action`, `enrich the problem-solving process`, `lasting peace and prosperity`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a traditional Chinese art form being performed on an international stage.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a performer is presenting a traditional Chinese art form on an international stage, captivating an audience of diverse cultural backgrounds. The enthusiastic response of the spectators underscores the universal appeal of cultural expression.

This scene illustrates the power of art as a medium for cultural exchange. Traditional art forms, whether Chinese opera, calligraphy, or martial arts, carry the wisdom and values of generations. When shared with the world, they not only preserve cultural heritage but also create opportunities for dialogue and understanding. Moreover, the international appreciation of Chinese culture has grown significantly in recent years, reflecting a broader trend toward cultural inclusivity.

In conclusion, we should take pride in our cultural heritage and actively share it with the world. By presenting our traditions in innovative and accessible ways, we can foster greater cross-cultural appreciation and build bridges between nations.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `captivating an audience`, `universal appeal`, `cultural heritage`, `cultural inclusivity`
