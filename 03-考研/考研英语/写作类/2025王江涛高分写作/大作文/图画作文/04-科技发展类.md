## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a person using AI to write an essay while looking puzzled.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a student is sitting in front of a computer, using an AI tool to generate an essay. Although the screen shows a perfectly written piece, the student looks puzzled and uncertain. This thought-provoking image highlights the complex relationship between artificial intelligence and human learning.

Technology has undoubtedly revolutionized the way we access and process information. AI-powered tools offer unprecedented convenience, enabling users to accomplish tasks in minutes that once took hours. However, this convenience comes with significant concerns. Over-reliance on AI may erode critical thinking skills and diminish the value of original thought. When students use AI to complete assignments without genuine understanding, they miss the opportunity to develop essential cognitive abilities. Furthermore, the ethical implications of AI-generated content raise important questions about authenticity and intellectual integrity.

In my opinion, the key lies in striking a balance between leveraging technology and preserving human agency. We should view AI as a tool to augment our capabilities, not replace them. By using technology responsibly and maintaining our commitment to genuine learning, we can harness the benefits of the digital age while safeguarding the qualities that make us uniquely human.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `unprecedented convenience`, `over-reliance on`, `erode critical thinking`, `leveraging technology`, `augment our capabilities`, `safeguarding the qualities`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a robot and a human shaking hands, symbolizing human-machine cooperation.
**范文**:
As is symbolically illustrated in the picture, a robot and a human are shaking hands, with gears and digital circuits blending seamlessly with human fingerprints. This powerful image symbolizes the era of human-machine cooperation and coexistence.

The rapid development of robotics and artificial intelligence has transformed virtually every aspect of modern life. In manufacturing, robots have taken over repetitive and dangerous tasks, improving efficiency and worker safety. In healthcare, AI-powered diagnostic tools assist doctors in making more accurate diagnoses. While technology brings undeniable benefits, it also raises concerns about job displacement and the loss of human touch in service industries.

In conclusion, we should embrace technology while remaining mindful of its potential drawbacks. The future belongs not to humans or machines alone, but to those who can effectively collaborate with both. It is essential that we develop the skills to work alongside intelligent machines while preserving the creativity, empathy, and judgment that remain uniquely human.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `blending seamlessly`, `human-machine cooperation`, `job displacement`, `uniquely human`
