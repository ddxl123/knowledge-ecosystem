## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a polluted river on one side and a clean river on the other, with a factory in the middle.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a river is divided into two contrasting halves. On one side, the water is crystal clear with fish swimming freely; on the other, it is dark and polluted with dead fish floating on the surface. A factory stands between them, with pipes discharging waste into the water. This disturbing image reveals the devastating impact of industrial pollution on our water resources.

The deterioration of the environment can be attributed to several factors. First and foremost, many enterprises prioritize economic profits over environmental protection, discharging untreated waste into rivers and the atmosphere. Furthermore, the lack of strict environmental regulations and enforcement allows polluters to operate with impunity. If this trend continues, the consequences will be catastrophic — not only will ecosystems collapse, but public health will be severely compromised, and future generations will inherit a degraded planet.

It is high time that we took concrete actions to address this crisis. The government should strengthen environmental legislation and impose heavy penalties on polluters. Enterprises must adopt cleaner production technologies and fulfill their social responsibilities. As individuals, we should reduce waste, recycle resources, and support sustainable products. Only through joint efforts can we restore the balance between economic development and environmental protection, ensuring a livable planet for generations to come.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `crystal clear`, `discharging untreated waste`, `operate with impunity`, `cleaner production technologies`, `fulfill their social responsibilities`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a city skyline covered in smog versus a clear blue sky.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a city skyline is engulfed in thick smog, with buildings barely visible through the haze. In contrast, a small corner of the image shows what the same city could look like under a clear blue sky. This stark contrast serves as a powerful reminder of the air pollution crisis facing modern cities.

The root causes of air pollution are multifaceted. Vehicle emissions, industrial smoke, and the burning of fossil fuels are the primary contributors. The health consequences are alarming — respiratory diseases, cardiovascular problems, and even cancer have been linked to prolonged exposure to polluted air. Moreover, smog reduces visibility, disrupts transportation, and tarnishes a city's image, affecting both quality of life and economic development.

In conclusion, addressing air pollution requires a comprehensive approach. Governments should invest in renewable energy, promote public transportation, and enforce emission standards. Citizens should adopt green commuting habits and reduce energy consumption. Let us remember that clean air is not a luxury but a fundamental right, and it is our collective responsibility to protect it.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `engulfed in thick smog`, `stark contrast`, `multifaceted`, `prolonged exposure`, `comprehensive approach`
