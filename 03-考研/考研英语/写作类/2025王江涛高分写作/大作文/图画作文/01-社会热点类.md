## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2024年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing people gathered around a large screen displaying news, with one person walking away reading a newspaper.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a group of people are gathered around a large screen, absorbed in the latest news headlines, while one person walks away calmly, reading a traditional newspaper. This thought-provoking image highlights the contrast between modern digital media consumption and traditional reading habits.

Several factors can account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, the rapid advancement of technology has made digital media the dominant source of information for the majority. The convenience and immediacy of online news appeal to a generation accustomed to instant gratification. Furthermore, social media platforms have created echo chambers where people are constantly exposed to information that reinforces their existing beliefs. However, the person reading the newspaper represents a growing minority who value in-depth analysis and independent thinking over sensational headlines.

In my view, while digital media offers undeniable advantages in terms of accessibility and speed, we should not abandon the critical thinking and deep reading that traditional media fosters. Only by striking a balance between the two can we become truly well-informed citizens in the digital age.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `thought-provoking image`, `echo chambers`, `instant gratification`, `striking a balance`, `in-depth analysis`

### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing a person climbing a mountain with a caption about perseverance.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a young man is climbing a steep mountain, with sweat on his face but determination in his eyes. The caption reads: "The journey may be tough, but the view from the top is worth every step." This powerful image conveys the timeless value of perseverance and hard work.

The message conveyed by the picture is both profound and practical. In today's fast-paced society, many people seek shortcuts and instant success, forgetting that true achievement requires sustained effort and dedication. Take the development of scientific research as an example — every major breakthrough is the result of years, sometimes decades, of painstaking work. Moreover, the ability to persevere through difficulties is what separates the extraordinary from the ordinary.

In conclusion, this picture serves as a powerful reminder that success is not a destination but a journey. We should embrace challenges with courage and determination, for it is through overcoming obstacles that we grow and ultimately achieve our goals.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `determination in his eyes`, `sustained effort`, `painstaking work`, `separates the extraordinary from the ordinary`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing people glued to their smartphones while ignoring each other at a dinner table.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, a family is sitting at a dinner table, yet each member is engrossed in their smartphone, completely ignoring one another. The irony is striking: they are physically together but mentally worlds apart. This image highlights the growing concern about technology's impact on interpersonal relationships.

This phenomenon can be attributed to several factors. First and foremost, smartphones have become an indispensable part of modern life, offering endless entertainment and information at our fingertips. Additionally, the addictive nature of social media and mobile games keeps users glued to their screens for hours. However, the consequences of this behavior are alarming. Studies have shown that excessive screen time leads to decreased family communication, weakened emotional bonds, and even mental health issues among young people.

In my opinion, while technology has brought tremendous convenience to our lives, we must not allow it to replace genuine human connection. Families should establish "phone-free" meal times, and individuals should consciously cultivate face-to-face interactions. Only in this way can we harness the benefits of technology without sacrificing the warmth of human relationships.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `engrossed in`, `physically together but mentally worlds apart`, `indispensable part`, `harness the benefits`, `cultivate face-to-face interactions`
