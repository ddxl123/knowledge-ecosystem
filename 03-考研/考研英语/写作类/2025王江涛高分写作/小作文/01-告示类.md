# 告示类

## 写作框架
第一段：标题 + 活动/事件概况
第二段：具体信息（时间、地点、参与方式）
第三段：联系方式 + 欢迎参与

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. NOTICE / Announcement
2. In order to..., we are pleased to announce that...
3. A... will be held/organized by...

### 中间句
1. The event is scheduled to take place on... from... to... at...
2. All students/faculty members are welcome to participate.
3. Those who are interested are required to sign up before...

### 结尾句
1. For further information, please contact... at...
2. We look forward to your active participation.
3. Don't miss this wonderful opportunity!

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write a notice to recruit student volunteers for an international conference.
**范文**:
**NOTICE**

**Volunteers Wanted for International Academic Conference**

In order to ensure the smooth running of the upcoming International Conference on Artificial Intelligence, we are now recruiting student volunteers.

The conference will be held from October 15th to 17th at the Main Conference Hall. Volunteers are expected to assist with registration, guide participants, and provide translation services. Those who are interested are required to submit an application form to the Student Union Office (Room 301, Building A) before September 30th. All applicants should have good communication skills and a basic command of English.

For further information, please contact Li Ming at 123-4567-8900 or send an email to volunteer@university.edu.cn.

We look forward to your active participation!

Student Union
September 10, 2024

**亮点词汇/句型**: `smooth running`, `recruiting student volunteers`, `a basic command of`, `active participation`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a notice to announce a charity sale event on campus.
**范文**:
**NOTICE**

**Annual Charity Sale**

In order to raise funds for children in poverty-stricken areas, the Student Volunteer Association is pleased to announce that our Annual Charity Sale will be held next week.

The event is scheduled to take place on Saturday, November 20th, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. in front of the Student Center. All students and faculty members are welcome to donate items or make purchases. The items for sale include books, clothing, stationery, and handmade crafts. All proceeds will be donated to the Hope Project.

For further information or to donate items, please contact the Student Volunteer Association at svc@university.edu.cn.

Don't miss this wonderful opportunity to make a difference!

Student Volunteer Association
November 10, 2024
