## 写作框架

第二段：背景说明 + 具体内容
第三段：行动要求 + 期望

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing this memo to inform you that...
2. This memo is to announce/notify/remind...
3. I would like to draw your attention to...

### 中间句
1. As you may be aware, ...
2. Please note that the following changes will take effect from...
3. The details are as follows:

### 结尾句
1. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions.
2. Your cooperation in this matter is greatly appreciated.
3. Thank you for your attention to this matter.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write a memo to all staff about a change in working hours.
**范文**:
**MEMORANDUM**

To: All Staff Members
From: Li Ming, Human Resources Manager
Date: December 1, 2024
Subject: Change in Working Hours

I am writing this memo to inform you of an adjustment to the company's working hours, which will take effect from January 1, 2025.

As part of our ongoing efforts to improve work-life balance, the company has decided to implement a flexible working schedule. The new core working hours will be from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and employees may choose to start between 8:00 and 10:00 a.m. and finish accordingly, provided they complete the required eight hours per day. This policy applies to all departments except the Customer Service Department, which will maintain its current schedule.

Please note that a detailed guideline will be distributed via email by December 15th. Your cooperation in adhering to the new schedule is greatly appreciated. If you have any questions, please contact the HR Department at ext. 8001.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `take effect from`, `work-life balance`, `flexible working schedule`, `adhering to`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a memo to announce a team-building activity.
**范文**:
**MEMORANDUM**

To: All Members of the Marketing Department
From: Li Ming, Department Head
Date: March 15, 2025
Subject: Team-Building Activity

I am pleased to announce that a team-building activity has been arranged for our department. This memo is to provide you with the relevant details.

The activity will take place on Saturday, March 28th, at Green Valley Resort, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The program includes outdoor team challenges, a barbecue lunch, and an awards ceremony. Transportation will be provided; the bus will depart from the company lobby at 8:00 a.m. sharp.

All members are required to confirm their attendance by March 20th by replying to this email. Please note that participation is strongly encouraged, as this event aims to strengthen team cohesion and enhance collaboration.

Your cooperation in this matter is greatly appreciated.
