## 写作框架
第一段：表明辞职意向 + 感谢
第二段：辞职原因（可选）+ 工作回顾
第三段：做好交接 + 表达祝福

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to inform you of my decision to resign from my position as...
2. After careful consideration, I have decided to resign from my current position.
3. I am writing to formally notify you of my intention to leave my position as...

### 中间句
1. Although I have enjoyed working here, I have decided to pursue a different career path.
2. The reason for my resignation is that I wish to further my education.
3. I have greatly valued the opportunities for professional growth that this position has provided.

### 结尾句
1. I am willing to assist in the transition process and ensure a smooth handover.
2. I would like to express my sincere gratitude for the support and guidance I have received.
3. I wish the company continued success in the future.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2016年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to your boss to resign from your current position.
**范文**:
Dear Mr. Wang,

I am writing to formally notify you of my decision to resign from my position as Marketing Assistant, effective from next month. I have greatly enjoyed my time at the company and appreciate the opportunities I have been given.

After careful consideration, I have decided to pursue a Master's degree in Business Administration at a foreign university. The knowledge and experience I have gained here have been invaluable in shaping this decision. I am deeply grateful for the mentorship and professional development opportunities you have provided during my tenure.

I am fully committed to ensuring a smooth transition and am willing to train my replacement during the notice period. I wish the company and the team continued success.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `formally notify`, `effective from`, `invaluable in shaping`, `smooth transition`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to resign from a volunteer position due to time constraints.
**范文**:
Dear Ms. Liu,

I am writing to inform you of my decision to resign from my volunteer position at the Community Learning Center. It has been a truly rewarding experience, and I am grateful for the opportunity to contribute.

Unfortunately, the increasing demands of my final semester have made it difficult for me to dedicate sufficient time to this role. I have greatly valued the chance to work with the children and staff, and this experience has enriched my life in ways I never expected.

I am willing to assist in finding and training my replacement to ensure continuity of the programs. I wish the Center and all its members the very best.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
