## 写作框架
第一段：表达歉意 + 说明事由
第二段：解释原因（简要）+ 提出补救措施
第三段：再次致歉 + 承诺不再犯

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my sincere apology for...
2. I am terribly sorry that I failed to...
3. Please accept my heartfelt apology for...

### 中间句
1. The reason for my mistake is that... However, I fully understand that this is no excuse.
2. I would like to make up for it by...
3. To compensate for the inconvenience, I would like to...

### 结尾句
1. I sincerely hope you can accept my apology.
2. Once again, I apologize for any inconvenience I may have caused.
3. I guarantee that such a mistake will never happen again.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2021年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to apologize for being unable to keep an appointment.
**范文**:
Dear Professor Zhang,

I am writing to express my sincere apology for being unable to keep our appointment on Friday afternoon. I understand that your time is precious, and I deeply regret any inconvenience this may have caused.

Unfortunately, I was suddenly informed of an urgent academic meeting that I could not postpone. While I understand this is no excuse for the inconvenience, I assure you it was an unforeseen circumstance. To make up for it, I would like to reschedule our meeting at any time that is convenient for you next week.

Once again, I apologize for any trouble I have caused. I guarantee that such a situation will not occur in the future.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `keep our appointment`, `unforeseen circumstance`, `reschedule`, `at any time that is convenient for you`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to your friend to apologize for damaging a book you borrowed.
**范文**:
Dear Li Hua,

I am terribly sorry that I accidentally damaged the book I borrowed from you last month. Please accept my heartfelt apology for this careless mistake.

While reading the book at a café, I accidentally spilled coffee on several pages. I understand how much this book means to you, especially since it was a gift from your grandfather. To compensate for the damage, I have already ordered a new copy and would like to present it to you along with a handwritten note of apology.

I sincerely hope you can forgive me. I will be much more careful with borrowed items in the future.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
