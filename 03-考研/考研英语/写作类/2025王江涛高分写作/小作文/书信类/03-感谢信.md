## 写作框架
第一段：表达感谢 + 说明感谢原因
第二段：具体描述帮助/礼物/款待的内容及其意义
第三段：再次致谢 + 回馈承诺

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for...
2. I would like to take this opportunity to extend my sincere thanks to you for...
3. I am more than grateful for your generous help with...

### 中间句
1. Without your assistance, I would not have been able to...
2. Your kindness and support meant a great deal to me during...
3. It was your encouragement that gave me the confidence to...

### 结尾句
1. I sincerely hope I can repay your kindness in the near future.
2. Please accept my heartfelt gratitude once again.
3. Thank you again for everything you have done for me.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2024年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to express your gratitude to a librarian for her help.
**范文**:
Dear Ms. Wang,

I am writing to extend my heartfelt gratitude for your generous assistance during my research project. Your help was invaluable and made a significant difference to my work.

During my frequent visits to the library over the past semester, you patiently guided me through the database systems and helped me locate numerous hard-to-find references. It was your professionalism and dedication that enabled me to complete my thesis on time. Moreover, your warm encouragement during moments of frustration gave me the motivation to persevere.

Please accept my sincere thanks once again. I truly hope I can return your kindness someday.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `invaluable`, `hard-to-find references`, `professionalism and dedication`, `persevere`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to thank a host family for their hospitality during your stay.
**范文**:
Dear Mr. and Mrs. Johnson,

I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for the wonderful hospitality you showed me during my stay in London. The three weeks I spent with your family were truly unforgettable.

Not only did you provide me with a comfortable room and delicious meals, but you also took the time to show me around the city and introduced me to the local culture. It was your warmth and generosity that made me feel at home even in a foreign country. The experience has deepened my understanding of British culture and enriched my perspective on life.

Thank you again for your extraordinary kindness. I hope to have the opportunity to host you in China someday.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
