## 写作框架
第一段：自我介绍 + 询问目的
第二段：具体问题（2-3个）
第三段：表达感谢 + 期待回复

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to inquire about...
2. I would like to obtain some information about...
3. I am writing to see if it is possible for you to provide me with information regarding...

### 中间句
1. I would be most grateful if you could inform me of...
2. Could you please tell me...?
3. I would also like to know...

### 结尾句
1. Your prompt attention to this letter would be highly appreciated.
2. I am looking forward to your earliest reply.
3. Thank you for your time and consideration.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2018年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to a university to inquire about the admission process.
**范文**:
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a senior student from Peking University, and I am writing to inquire about the graduate admission process for the upcoming academic year at your esteemed institution.

I would be most grateful if you could provide me with the following information. First, I would like to know the specific requirements for international applicants, including language proficiency scores and academic prerequisites. Furthermore, could you please inform me of the application deadline and the necessary documents? I would also like to know whether there are any scholarships available for international students.

Your prompt attention to this letter would be highly appreciated. I am looking forward to your earliest reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `esteemed institution`, `language proficiency scores`, `academic prerequisites`, `prompt attention`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to a company to inquire about a summer internship opportunity.
**范文**:
Dear Human Resources Manager,

I am a junior student majoring in Computer Science, and I am writing to inquire about summer internship opportunities at your company.

I would be most grateful if you could provide me with several details. First, I would like to know the available positions and their specific requirements. Additionally, could you please inform me of the application process and deadline? I would also appreciate it if you could let me know whether the internship offers any compensation or accommodation.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I am looking forward to your earliest reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
