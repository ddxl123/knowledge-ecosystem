## 写作框架
第一段：说明推荐对象 + 与被推荐人的关系
第二段：推荐理由（能力、性格、成就）
第三段：总结推荐 + 表达信心

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to recommend... to you without reservation.
2. It is with great pleasure that I recommend... for...
3. I take great pleasure in recommending... as...

### 中间句
1. During the time I worked with him/her, I was deeply impressed by...
2. He/She possesses outstanding qualities such as...
3. What distinguishes him/her from others is...

### 结尾句
1. I am confident that he/she will be an excellent candidate for...
2. I strongly recommend... and believe he/she will exceed your expectations.
3. Should you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2026年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to recommend a book to a foreign friend.
**范文**:
Dear Tom,

I am writing to recommend a book to you that I believe you will find both insightful and enjoyable. The book, titled "Erta Ale," is a fascinating exploration of Chinese culture and history.

This book stands out for several reasons. First and foremost, it offers a vivid and accessible introduction to Chinese civilization spanning over five thousand years. Furthermore, the author's engaging writing style makes complex cultural concepts easy to understand. What distinguishes this book from others is its combination of academic rigor and storytelling, which keeps readers thoroughly engaged throughout.

I am confident that you will find this book both educational and entertaining. Should you have any questions about it, please feel free to reach out.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `insightful and enjoyable`, `accessible introduction`, `academic rigor`, `engaging writing style`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to a professor to recommend a classmate for a research assistant position.
**范文**:
Dear Professor Wang,

I take great pleasure in recommending my classmate, Zhang Wei, for the research assistant position in your laboratory. Having studied alongside him for three years, I can attest to his exceptional abilities.

Zhang Wei possesses outstanding qualities that make him an ideal candidate. His strong analytical skills and meticulous attention to detail have earned him the highest grades in our research methodology course. Moreover, his collaborative spirit and excellent communication skills make him a valued team member. It was his dedication to academic excellence that earned him the National Scholarship last year.

I strongly recommend Zhang Wei and am confident he will exceed your expectations. Please do not hesitate to contact me for further information.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
