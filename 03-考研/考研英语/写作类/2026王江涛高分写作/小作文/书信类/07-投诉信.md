# 投诉信

## 写作框架
第一段：自我介绍 + 投诉对象
第二段：具体问题描述 + 影响/后果
第三段：提出解决方案/要求 + 期望回复

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with...
2. I regret to inform you that I am not satisfied with...
3. I am writing to lodge a formal complaint regarding...

### 中间句
1. The problem is that... which has caused me considerable inconvenience.
2. Not only... but also... This situation is simply unacceptable.
3. To make matters worse, ... despite my repeated attempts to...

### 结尾句
1. I would appreciate it if you could look into this matter and resolve it as soon as possible.
2. I trust you will give this matter your immediate attention.
3. Unless this problem is resolved promptly, I will have no choice but to...

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2019年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to a customer service department to complain about a defective product.
**范文**:
Dear Customer Service Manager,

I am writing to lodge a formal complaint regarding a laptop computer I purchased from your online store on March 15th (Order No. 202403150078).

Upon receiving the product, I discovered that the screen displayed intermittent flickering, and the battery failed to hold a charge for more than two hours, far below the advertised eight hours. To make matters worse, the keyboard became unresponsive after just one week of use. I have contacted your support hotline three times, but the issue remains unresolved.

I would appreciate it if you could arrange for a replacement or a full refund at your earliest convenience. I trust you will give this matter your immediate attention.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `lodge a formal complaint`, `intermittent flickering`, `far below the advertised`, `at your earliest convenience`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to the university administration to complain about the poor condition of the dormitory.
**范文**:
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with the living conditions in Dormitory Building 5. As a resident for the past two years, I feel compelled to bring several issues to your attention.

The most pressing problem is the frequent water supply disruption, which occurs almost every morning. Additionally, the heating system has been malfunctioning throughout the winter, making the rooms uncomfortably cold. To make matters worse, the internet connection has been extremely unstable, severely affecting our academic work.

I would appreciate it if you could investigate these issues and implement necessary repairs as soon as possible. I trust that the administration will prioritize the well-being of its students.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
