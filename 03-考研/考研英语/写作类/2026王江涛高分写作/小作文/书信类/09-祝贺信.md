## 写作框架
第一段：表达祝贺 + 说明祝贺原因
第二段：对对方成就的肯定 + 具体赞美
第三段：表达未来祝愿

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my heartfelt congratulations on...
2. It is with great pleasure that I congratulate you on...
3. Congratulations on your remarkable achievement in...

### 中间句
1. Your success is well deserved, given your hard work and dedication.
2. No one deserves this honor more than you.
3. It is a recognition of your years of effort and perseverance.

### 结尾句
1. I wish you every success in your future endeavors.
2. May your future be filled with even greater accomplishments.
3. I am confident that you will continue to achieve remarkable results.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2017年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to congratulate a friend on being admitted to a graduate program.
**范文**:
Dear Zhang Wei,

I am writing to express my heartfelt congratulations on your admission to the Master's program in Economics at Tsinghua University. This is truly a remarkable achievement that you should be extremely proud of.

Your success is well deserved, given your unwavering dedication to your studies over the past four years. I still remember the countless nights you spent in the library preparing for the entrance examination. No one deserves this honor more than you, and I have no doubt that you will continue to excel in your graduate studies.

May your future be filled with even greater accomplishments. I wish you every success in this exciting new chapter of your life.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `remarkable achievement`, `unwavering dedication`, `deserve this honor`, `exciting new chapter`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to congratulate a colleague on winning a national award.
**范文**:
Dear Mr. Chen,

It is with great pleasure that I congratulate you on winning the National Science and Technology Innovation Award. This prestigious recognition is a testament to your outstanding contributions to the field of renewable energy.

Your groundbreaking research on solar cell efficiency has not only advanced scientific knowledge but also brought practical benefits to millions of people. It is a recognition of your years of tireless effort and remarkable perseverance in the face of challenges. Your achievement is an inspiration to all of us in the research community.

I wish you continued success in all your future endeavors. May this award be just the beginning of even greater achievements to come.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
