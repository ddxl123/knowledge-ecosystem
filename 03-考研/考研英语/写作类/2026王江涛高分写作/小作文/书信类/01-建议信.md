# 建议信

## 写作框架
第一段：自我介绍 + 写信目的（提出建议）
第二段：具体建议（2-3条），每条配理由
第三段：表达期待 + 礼貌收尾

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to express my suggestions concerning...
2. I am more than delighted to have the opportunity to make some suggestions regarding...
3. As a regular customer / student / citizen, I would like to offer the following suggestions.

### 中间句
1. My suggestions are as follows. In the first place, it is advisable for you to... In the second place, I propose that...
2. First and foremost, I would like to suggest that... Furthermore, it would be beneficial if...
3. To begin with, I strongly recommend that... Additionally, ... deserves your attention.

### 结尾句
1. I sincerely hope you will find these suggestions helpful.
2. I would be grateful if you could take my suggestions into consideration.
3. Thank you for your time and attention, and I am looking forward to your favorable reply.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to a professor, making suggestions for his course.
**范文**:
Dear Professor Smith,

I am writing to offer several suggestions regarding the course you are teaching. As a student who has greatly benefited from your lectures, I hope my humble suggestions could help improve the learning experience.

First and foremost, I would like to suggest that more practical exercises be incorporated into the course. Many students, myself included, find it challenging to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems. Furthermore, it would be highly beneficial if you could recommend some supplementary reading materials after each lecture. This would enable us to explore the topics in greater depth.

Thank you sincerely for your time and consideration. I am confident that with these adjustments, the course would become even more rewarding for all students.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `benefited from`, `incorporated into`, `in greater depth`, `supplementary reading materials`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write a letter to the editor of a newspaper, suggesting ways to protect the environment.
**范文**:
Dear Editor,

I am writing to express my suggestions concerning the environmental protection measures reported in your recent article. As a citizen deeply concerned about ecological issues, I would like to propose several approaches.

First and foremost, I strongly recommend that the government impose stricter regulations on industrial pollution. Additionally, public awareness campaigns should be launched to encourage citizens to adopt eco-friendly lifestyles. It would also be beneficial if corporations were incentivized to invest in renewable energy sources.

I sincerely hope these suggestions will prove valuable. Thank you for your attention to this critical issue.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming
