## 写作框架
第一段：描述折线图数据 + 总体趋势
第二段：分析变化原因/趋势
第三段：总结 + 预测/建议

## 万能句型
### 开头句（描述数据）
1. As is clearly illustrated in the line graph, ... showed a steady/sharp increase/decrease from... to...
2. The line graph reveals that the trend of... fluctuated significantly between... and...
3. According to the line graph, ... witnessed a dramatic rise from... to..., followed by a slight decline.

### 中间句（分析趋势）
1. The upward/downward trend can be explained by several factors.
2. One possible explanation for this fluctuation is...
3. The data reflects the profound impact of... on...

### 结尾句（总结预测）
1. If this trend continues, it is likely that...
2. In summary, the line graph indicates a clear shift from... to...
3. Policymakers should pay close attention to this trend and take appropriate measures.

## 真题范文（2020-2026）
### 预测题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a line graph showing the changes in mobile payment usage in China from 2016 to 2024.
**范文**:
As is clearly illustrated in the line graph, mobile payment usage in China experienced a remarkable surge from 2016 to 2024. The percentage of consumers using mobile payment rose sharply from 40% in 2016 to 78% in 2020, and continued to climb to 92% by 2024, though the rate of growth slowed in the later years.

Several factors can explain this upward trend. First and foremost, the widespread adoption of smartphones and the improvement of internet infrastructure laid the foundation for mobile payment. Furthermore, the convenience and security offered by platforms such as Alipay and WeChat Pay attracted an ever-growing number of users. The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 also accelerated the shift to contactless payment methods. However, the slowing growth rate in recent years suggests that the market is approaching saturation, and further expansion will require innovation and the development of new use cases.

In summary, the line graph indicates a fundamental transformation in China's payment landscape. Policymakers should continue to strengthen cybersecurity measures and promote financial inclusion to ensure that the benefits of mobile payment are accessible to all segments of society.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `remarkable surge`, `widespread adoption`, `contactless payment`, `market saturation`, `financial inclusion`

## 预测范文
### 预测题1
**题目**: Write an essay based on a line graph showing the changes in global carbon emissions from 2010 to 2024.
**范文**:
As is clearly illustrated in the line graph, global carbon emissions followed an alarming trajectory between 2010 and 2024. Emissions rose steadily from 33 billion metric tons in 2010 to 37 billion in 2019. After a brief dip to 34 billion in 2020 due to pandemic-related lockdowns, emissions rebounded sharply, reaching 38 billion metric tons by 2024.

The persistent rise in carbon emissions can be attributed to several factors. The continued reliance on fossil fuels for energy production remains the primary driver. Additionally, rapid industrialization in developing countries has contributed to increased emissions. The temporary decline in 2020 demonstrated that behavioral changes can significantly reduce emissions, but the subsequent rebound highlights the need for structural changes in energy systems rather than temporary measures.

In conclusion, the data underscores the urgent need for comprehensive climate action. Governments worldwide must accelerate the transition to renewable energy, implement carbon pricing mechanisms, and invest in green technologies. If this upward trend is not reversed, the consequences for our planet will be severe and potentially irreversible.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `alarming trajectory`, `reliance on fossil fuels`, `structural changes`, `comprehensive climate action`, `irreversible`
