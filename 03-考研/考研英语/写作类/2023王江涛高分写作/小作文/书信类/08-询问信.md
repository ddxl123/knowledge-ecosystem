# 询问信

## 写作框架
第一段：自我介绍 + 询问目的
第二段：具体问题（2-3个）
第三段：表达感谢 + 期望回复

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to inquire about...
2. I would like to obtain some information about...
3. I am writing to see if it is possible for you to provide me with information regarding...

### 中间句
1. Could you please inform me of...?
2. I would also like to know...
3. In addition, I wonder if you could tell me...

### 结尾句
1. Thank you for your time and attention.
2. I look forward to your early reply.
3. Your prompt attention to this letter would be highly appreciated.

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2020年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to inquire about a summer training program.
**范文**:
Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to inquire about the summer training program offered by your institution. As a third-year university student majoring in Computer Science, I am very interested in participating in this program.

I would be grateful if you could provide me with the following information. First, could you please inform me of the specific dates and duration of the program? Second, I would also like to know whether there are any scholarship opportunities available for outstanding students. In addition, I wonder if you could tell me the application deadline and the required documents.

Thank you very much for your time and attention. I look forward to your early reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `inquire about`, `participating in`, `scholarship opportunities`, `required documents`
