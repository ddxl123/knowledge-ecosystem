# 辞职信

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
2023王江涛高分写作小作文书信类中辞职信的写作指导，包含格式规范、万能句型和范文。

### 知识结构
- **格式规范**：书信的标准格式
- **万能句型**：开头、正文、结尾的常用表达
- **范文**：高分范文及赏析

### 待收集原子知识点
- 书信格式：辞职信的标准格式规范
- 万能句型：开头问候、正文表达、结尾祝愿的常用句型
- 范文分析：高分范文的结构和语言特点
## 写作框架
第一段：表明辞职决定 + 辞职原因
第二段：对工作的感恩 + 交接安排
第三段：表达歉意 + 祝愿公司

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to inform you of my decision to resign from my position as...
2. After careful consideration, I have decided to resign from...
3. It is with great reluctance that I submit my resignation.

### 中间句
1. I have thoroughly enjoyed working with the team and appreciate the opportunities I have been given.
2. To ensure a smooth transition, I am willing to...
3. The reason for my resignation is that...

### 结尾句
1. I apologize for any inconvenience my departure may cause.
2. I wish the company continued success and growth.
3. Thank you for the support and guidance during my tenure.

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to your manager to resign from a part-time job.
**范文**:
Dear Mr. Johnson,

I am writing to inform you of my decision to resign from my position as a part-time research assistant, effective from next month. After careful consideration, I have concluded that I need to focus more on my final-year thesis and upcoming graduate entrance examination.

I have thoroughly enjoyed working with the research team over the past year. The experience has been incredibly enriching, and I am deeply grateful for the guidance and support you have provided. To ensure a smooth transition, I am willing to train my replacement and complete any pending tasks before my departure.

I apologize for any inconvenience my departure may cause and wish the team continued success in its future research endeavors.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `effective from`, `incredibly enriching`, `smooth transition`, `pending tasks`
