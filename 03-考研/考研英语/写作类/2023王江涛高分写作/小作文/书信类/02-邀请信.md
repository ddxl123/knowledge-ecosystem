## 写作框架
第一段：自我介绍 + 邀请目的
第二段：活动详情（时间、地点、内容）
第三段：期待回复 + 表达诚意

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. I am writing to invite you to...
2. It is with great pleasure that I invite you to attend...
3. On behalf of ..., I would like to extend a warm invitation to you.

### 中间句
1. The event is scheduled to take place on [date] at [venue].
2. During the event, you will have the opportunity to...
3. We have arranged a series of activities, including...

### 结尾句
1. I sincerely hope you can accept my invitation.
2. Your presence would be a great honor for us.
3. I am looking forward to your favorable reply at your earliest convenience.

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write a letter to invite an international student to join a cultural event.
**范文**:
Dear friend,

I am writing to invite you to participate in the Chinese Cultural Festival organized by our university. As an international student, I believe this event would be a wonderful opportunity for you to experience traditional Chinese culture firsthand.

The festival is scheduled to take place on November 15th at the university auditorium. During the event, you will have the opportunity to enjoy traditional Chinese calligraphy demonstrations, taste authentic Chinese cuisine, and watch performances of Peking Opera. We have also arranged a workshop where you can learn to make Chinese paper-cutting art.

Your presence would greatly enrich the diversity of our event. I sincerely hope you can accept my invitation and look forward to seeing you there.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

**亮点词汇/句型**: `participate in`, `firsthand`, `authentic`, `enrich the diversity`
