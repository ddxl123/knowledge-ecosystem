## 写作框架
格式：MEMO
TO：收件人
FROM：发件人
DATE：日期
SUBJECT：主题

正文：
第一段：目的/背景
第二段：具体事项
第三段：行动要求

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. This memo is to inform you that...
2. I am writing this memo to...
3. The purpose of this memo is to...

### 中间句
1. Please note the following details...
2. It has been decided that...
3. The following measures will be taken...

### 结尾句
1. Please confirm your understanding by...
2. Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to...
3. Your cooperation in this matter is greatly appreciated.

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2023年真题
**题目**: Write a memo to inform colleagues about a change in meeting schedule.
**范文**:

MEMO

TO: All Department Staff
FROM: Li Ming, Department Manager
DATE: March 10, 2023
SUBJECT: Change of Weekly Meeting Schedule

This memo is to inform you that the weekly department meeting, originally scheduled for every Monday at 9:00 AM, has been rescheduled to every Wednesday at 2:00 PM, effective from next week.

The change is due to the new project coordination meetings that will now take place on Monday mornings. Please note the following details: the Wednesday meetings will be held in Conference Room 305, and each meeting is expected to last approximately one hour.

Please update your calendars accordingly. Should you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact me. Your cooperation in this matter is greatly appreciated.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `rescheduled`, `effective from`, `coordination meetings`, `update your calendars accordingly`
