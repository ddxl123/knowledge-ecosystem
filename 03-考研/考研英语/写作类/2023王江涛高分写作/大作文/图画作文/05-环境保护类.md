# 环境保护类（图画作文）

## 写作框架
第一段：描述图画 + 环境问题
第二段：分析原因/危害
第三段：提出对策 + 呼吁行动

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is vividly depicted in the picture, ...
2. The picture presents a disturbing scene that highlights the urgency of environmental protection.
3. This drawing serves as a stark warning about the consequences of environmental degradation.

### 中间句
1. The root cause of this problem lies in...
2. If left unchecked, this trend will lead to catastrophic consequences.
3. Environmental protection is no longer a choice but a necessity.

### 结尾句
1. It is high time that we took concrete actions to protect our environment.
2. Only by making joint efforts can we leave a green planet for future generations.
3. Let us work together to build a sustainable and livable world.

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture showing the contrast between a polluted river and a clean river.
**范文**:
As is vividly depicted in the picture, two contrasting scenes of the same river are presented: on the left, the water is dark and polluted, with dead fish floating on the surface; on the right, the river is crystal clear, with children playing happily by the banks. This drawing serves as a stark warning about the consequences of environmental degradation.

The root cause of water pollution lies in the unchecked discharge of industrial waste and sewage. If left untreated, contaminated water will not only destroy aquatic ecosystems but also pose serious threats to public health. Moreover, the cost of environmental remediation far exceeds the cost of prevention. Environmental protection is no longer a choice but a necessity for the survival of our species.

It is high time that we took concrete actions to protect our water resources. Governments should impose stricter regulations on pollutant discharge, while individuals should reduce their ecological footprint in daily life. Only by making joint efforts can we restore the beauty of our rivers and leave a green planet for future generations.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `crystal clear`, `unchecked discharge`, `pose serious threats`, `ecological footprint`
