第三段：总结 + 呼吁践行

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is symbolically depicted in the picture, ...
2. The picture vividly illustrates the importance of...
3. What the picture conveys is far beyond what words can describe.

### 中间句
1. The quality of ... plays a crucial role in one's personal growth.
2. History is replete with examples that demonstrate the power of...
3. It is universally acknowledged that ... is the key to success.

### 结尾句
1. In a word, we should cultivate the spirit of ... in our daily life.
2. Let us take action from now on and make ... a part of our character.
3. Only by possessing this quality can we lead a meaningful and fulfilling life.

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a picture illustrating the value of teamwork.
**范文**:
As is symbolically depicted in the picture, several people are rowing a boat together, each contributing their strength in perfect coordination. The caption reads: "Unity is strength." This vivid image illustrates the crucial importance of teamwork in achieving common goals.

The quality of cooperation plays a vital role in both personal and professional life. In the workplace, no significant project can be completed by a single individual — it requires the collective effort of a team. Take China's space program as an example: the successful launch of the Shenzhou spacecraft was the result of thousands of scientists and engineers working together toward a shared vision. History is replete with examples that demonstrate the power of collaboration.

In a word, we should cultivate the spirit of teamwork in our daily life. Let us learn to appreciate others' strengths, respect different opinions, and contribute our unique talents to the collective. Only by working together can we overcome challenges and achieve greater success.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `in perfect coordination`, `collective effort`, `shared vision`, `cultivate the spirit of teamwork`
