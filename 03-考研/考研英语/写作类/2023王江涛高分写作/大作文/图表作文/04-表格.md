# 表格（图表作文）

## 核心概念

### 条目描述
2023王江涛高分写作大作文图表作文中表格的写作指导，包含写作框架、万能句型和范文。

### 知识结构
- **写作框架**：三段式结构（描述图表→分析原因→总结评论）
- **万能句型**：图表描述、数据分析的常用表达
- **范文**：高分范文及赏析

### 待收集原子知识点
- 写作框架：表格类图表作文的三段式结构
- 图表描述句型：描述表格的常用表达
- 数据分析表达：分析数据趋势的句型
- 范文分析：高分范文的结构和语言特点
## 写作框架
第一段：描述表格数据 + 对比关键信息
第二段：分析数据差异的原因
第三段：总结 + 建议

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is clearly presented in the table, ...
2. The table compares ... across different categories/years/regions.
3. According to the table, there are notable differences in...

### 中间句
1. The gap between ... and ... can be explained by...
2. A closer look at the data reveals that...
3. The most striking difference lies in...

### 结尾句
1. In conclusion, the data suggests that...
2. To bridge the gap, it is essential that...
3. These findings have important implications for...

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2020年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a table showing the employment rates of graduates from different majors.
**范文**:
As is clearly presented in the table, the employment rates of college graduates vary significantly across different majors. Computer Science and Engineering lead with rates above 90%, while Humanities and Arts trail behind at approximately 65%. Business and Economics fall in the middle range at around 80%.

A closer look at the data reveals that the gap is closely linked to market demand and practical skills. STEM graduates possess technical competencies that are highly sought after in the digital economy, giving them a competitive edge in the job market. In contrast, Humanities graduates often face a mismatch between their academic training and employer expectations. The most striking difference lies in starting salaries, with STEM graduates earning nearly double that of their Humanities counterparts.

In conclusion, the data suggests that universities should strengthen practical training and career guidance, particularly for students in less marketable fields. To bridge the gap, it is essential that educational institutions adapt their curricula to meet the evolving needs of the job market.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `vary significantly`, `competitive edge`, `mismatch between`, `evolving needs`
