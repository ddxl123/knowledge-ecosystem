## 写作框架
第一段：描述饼图各部分比例 + 总体特征

第三段：总结 + 建议

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is clearly illustrated in the pie chart, ...
2. The pie chart provides a breakdown of ...
3. According to the pie chart, ... accounts for the largest proportion of...

### 中间句
1. The dominant position of ... can be attributed to...
2. In comparison, ... represents only a small fraction.
3. The disparity between ... and ... reflects...

### 结尾句
1. In conclusion, the distribution reveals that...
2. To achieve a more balanced distribution, it is suggested that...
3. This pattern is likely to evolve as...

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2022年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a pie chart showing how college students spend their free time.
**范文**:
As is clearly illustrated in the pie chart, college students' free time is distributed across various activities. Social media and online entertainment account for the largest proportion at 35%, followed by academic study at 25%. Physical exercise and sports make up 15%, while part-time jobs and social activities each represent 12.5%.

The dominant position of social media can be attributed to the widespread use of smartphones and the addictive nature of short-video platforms. Many students find it difficult to resist the temptation of scrolling through endless content. In comparison, the relatively low proportion of physical exercise reflects a growing concern about sedentary lifestyles among young people. The disparity between entertainment and exercise highlights the need for better time management.

In conclusion, the distribution reveals an imbalanced pattern that warrants attention. To achieve a healthier and more productive lifestyle, students should consciously limit screen time and allocate more time to physical activity and face-to-face social interactions.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `accounts for the largest proportion`, `addictive nature`, `sedentary lifestyles`, `warrants attention`
