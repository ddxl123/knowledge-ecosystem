# 折线图（图表作文）

## 写作框架
第一段：描述折线图趋势 + 数据变化
第二段：分析原因/影响因素
第三段：总结 + 展望

## 万能句型
### 开头句
1. As is clearly depicted in the line graph, ...
2. The line graph shows the changing trend of ... over the period from ... to...
3. It can be seen from the line graph that ... experienced a dramatic/gradual change.

### 中间句
1. The upward/downward trend can be explained by...
2. Several factors account for this fluctuation.
3. The data reveals a correlation between ... and...

### 结尾句
1. In summary, the trend indicates that...
2. If this trend continues, we can expect...
3. It is imperative that effective measures be taken to...

## 真题范文（2020-2023）
### 2021年真题
**题目**: Write an essay based on a line graph showing the number of Chinese tourists traveling abroad from 2015 to 2023.
**范文**:
As is clearly depicted in the line graph, the number of Chinese tourists traveling abroad has shown a steady upward trend from 2015 to 2019, rising from 120 million to 170 million. However, there was a sharp decline in 2020 due to the pandemic, dropping to approximately 30 million. The figure has been gradually recovering since 2022.

Several factors account for this fluctuation. The pre-pandemic growth was driven by rising disposable incomes, relaxed visa policies, and the increasing desire for cultural exploration among Chinese citizens. The dramatic decline in 2020 was a direct result of global travel restrictions and health concerns. The recent recovery reflects the successful containment of the pandemic and the pent-up demand for international travel.

In summary, the trend indicates that outbound tourism from China will continue to grow as global conditions stabilize. However, the industry must adapt to new consumer preferences, including a greater emphasis on safety, flexibility, and experiential travel.

**亮点词汇/句型**: `steady upward trend`, `sharp decline`, `pent-up demand`, `experiential travel`
