## objective
- **音标**: /əbˈdʒektɪv/
- **词性**: n. adj.
- **释义**: 目标；客观的
- **例句**: The main objective of the project is to reduce costs.
- **近义词**: goal, aim, target
- **派生词**: objectively (adv.), objectivity (n.), subjective (反义)

## obligation
- **音标**: /ˌɑːblɪˈɡeɪʃn/

- **释义**: 义务；责任

- **近义词**: duty, responsibility, commitment
- **派生词**: obligate (v.), obligatory (adj.)
