## commit
- **音标**: /kəˈmɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 犯（罪）；承诺；委托；投入
- **例句**: He committed a serious crime and was sent to prison.
- **近义词**: perpetrate, pledge, dedicate
- **派生词**: commitment (n.), committee (n.), committed (adj.)
