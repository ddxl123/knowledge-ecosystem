## draft
- **音标**: /dræft/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 草稿；草案；起草
- **例句**: She submitted the first draft of her essay.
- **近义词**: sketch, outline, compose
- **派生词**: drafting (n.), drafter (n.)

## drag
- **音标**: /dræɡ/

- **近义词**: pull, haul, tow
- **派生词**: dragging (adj.)
