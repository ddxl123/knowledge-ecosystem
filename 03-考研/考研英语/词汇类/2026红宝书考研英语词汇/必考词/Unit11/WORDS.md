## fabric
- **音标**: /ˈfæbrɪk/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 织物；布料；结构
- **例句**: The fabric of society is built on trust and cooperation.
- **近义词**: cloth, textile, material
- **派生词**: fabricate (v.)

## facilitate
- **音标**: /fəˈsɪlɪteɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 促进；使便利
- **例句**: The new software facilitates communication between teams.
- **近义词**: ease, promote, enable
- **派生词**: facilitation (n.), facilitator (n.), facility (n.)

## factor
