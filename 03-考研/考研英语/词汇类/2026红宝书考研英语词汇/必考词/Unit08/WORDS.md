# Unit 08 单词

## deprive
- **音标**: /dɪˈpraɪv/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 剥夺；使丧失
- **例句**: The war deprived many children of their education.
- **近义词**: strip, rob, deny
- **派生词**: deprivation (n.), deprived (adj.)
