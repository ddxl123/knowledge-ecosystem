## considerable
- **音标**: /kənˈsɪdərəbl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 相当大的；重要的
- **例句**: A considerable amount of money was spent on the project.
- **近义词**: significant, substantial, sizable
- **派生词**: considerably (adv.)

## consist
- **音标**: /kənˈsɪst/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 由…组成；在于
- **例句**: The team consists of ten members.
- **近义词**: comprise, include, lie in
- **派生词**: consistent (adj.), consistency (n.), inconsistent (adj.)

## constant
