## rival
- **音标**: /ˈraɪvl/
- **词性**: n. adj. v.
- **释义**: 竞争对手；对抗
- **例句**: The two companies have been rivals for decades.
- **近义词**: competitor, opponent, adversary
- **派生词**: rivalry (n.)
