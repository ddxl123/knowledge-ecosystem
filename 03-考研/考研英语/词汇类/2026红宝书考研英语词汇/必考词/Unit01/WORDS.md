## abandon
- **音标**: /əˈbændən/
- **词性**: v. n.
- **释义**: 放弃；抛弃；放纵
- **例句**: He abandoned his plan to travel abroad.
- **近义词**: desert, forsake, give up
- **派生词**: abandonment (n.), abandoned (adj.)
