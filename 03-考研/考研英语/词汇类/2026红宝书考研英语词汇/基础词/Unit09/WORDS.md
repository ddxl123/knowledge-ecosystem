# Unit 09 单词

## earnest
- **音标**: /ˈɜːrnɪst/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 认真的；诚挚的
- **例句**: He made an earnest effort to improve his grades.
- **近义词**: sincere, serious, dedicated
- **派生词**: earnestly (adv.), earnestness (n.)

## ease
- **音标**: /iːz/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 容易；舒适；减轻
- **例句**: He solved the problem with ease.
- **近义词**: simplicity, comfort, relieve
- **派生词**: easy (adj.), easily (adv.), uneasy (adj.)
