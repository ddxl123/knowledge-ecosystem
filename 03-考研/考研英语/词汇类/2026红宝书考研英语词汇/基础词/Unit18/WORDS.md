# Unit 18 单词

## native
- **音标**: /ˈneɪtɪv/
- **词性**: adj. n.
- **释义**: 本地的；土著的
- **例句**: She is a native speaker of English.
- **近义词**: local, indigenous, born
- **派生词**: natively (adv.)

## navigate
- **音标**: /ˈnævɪɡeɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 导航；航行；穿越
- **例句**: It's difficult to navigate through the heavy traffic.
- **近义词**: steer, direct, find one's way
- **派生词**: navigation (n.), navigator (n.)

## neat
- **音标**: /niːt/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 整洁的；简洁的；巧妙的
- **例句**: Her handwriting is very neat and easy to read.
- **近义词**: tidy, clean, organized
- **派生词**: neatly (adv.), neatness (n.)

## negative
- **音标**: /ˈneɡətɪv/
- **词性**: adj. n.
- **释义**: 否定的；消极的；负的
- **例句**: Don't be so negative about everything.
- **近义词**: pessimistic, unfavorable, adverse
- **派生词**: negatively (adv.), negate (v.), positive (反义)

## neglect

- **近义词**: ignore, overlook, disregard
- **派生词**: neglectful (adj.), negligible (adj.)
