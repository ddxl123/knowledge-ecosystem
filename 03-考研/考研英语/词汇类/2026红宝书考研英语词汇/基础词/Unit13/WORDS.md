## generous
- **音标**: /ˈdʒenərəs/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 慷慨的；大量的；宽厚的
- **例句**: She is very generous with her time and money.
- **近义词**: liberal, charitable, giving
- **派生词**: generosity (n.), generously (adv.)

## genius
- **音标**: /ˈdʒiːniəs/

- **释义**: 天才；天赋
- **例句**: Einstein was a genius in physics.
- **近义词**: brilliance, talent, prodigy
