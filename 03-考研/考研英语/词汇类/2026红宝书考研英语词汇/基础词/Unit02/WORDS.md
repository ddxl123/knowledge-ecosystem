## ancestor
- **音标**: /ˈænsestər/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 祖先；祖宗
- **例句**: Our ancestors lived in caves thousands of years ago.
- **近义词**: forefather, predecessor
- **派生词**: ancestral (adj.), ancestry (n.)

- **近义词**: perspective, viewpoint, corner
- **派生词**: angular (adj.)
