## require
- **音标**: /rɪˈkwaɪər/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 需要；要求
- **例句**: This job requires a lot of patience.
- **近义词**: need, demand, necessitate
- **派生词**: requirement (n.), required (adj.)

- **近义词**: study, investigation, inquiry
- **派生词**: researcher (n.)
