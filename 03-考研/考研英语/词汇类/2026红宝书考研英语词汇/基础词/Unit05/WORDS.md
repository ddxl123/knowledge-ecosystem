## comprehensive
- **音标**: /ˌkɑːmprɪˈhensɪv/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 综合的；全面的
- **例句**: The report provides a comprehensive analysis of the situation.
- **近义词**: thorough, complete, extensive
- **派生词**: comprehensively (adv.), comprehension (n.)

## compromise
- **音标**: /ˈkɑːmprəmaɪz/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 妥协；折中；损害
- **例句**: They reached a compromise after long negotiations.
- **近义词**: concession, settlement, agreement
- **派生词**: compromising (adj.)
