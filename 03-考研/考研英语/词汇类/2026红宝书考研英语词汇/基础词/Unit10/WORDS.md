## equip
- **音标**: /ɪˈkwɪp/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 装备；配备；使有能力
- **例句**: The school is well equipped with modern technology.
- **近义词**: furnish, supply, provide
- **派生词**: equipment (n.)

## equivalent
- **音标**: /ɪˈkwɪvələnt/
- **词性**: adj. n.
- **释义**: 等同的；对等的人或物
- **例句**: What is the equivalent word in Chinese?
- **近义词**: equal, comparable, corresponding
- **派生词**: equivalence (n.)

## error
- **音标**: /ˈerər/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 错误；过失

- **派生词**: erroneous (adj.), err (v.)
