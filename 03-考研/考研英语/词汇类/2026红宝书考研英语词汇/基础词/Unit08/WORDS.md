## device
- **音标**: /dɪˈvaɪs/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 设备；装置；手段
- **例句**: This device can measure air quality.
- **近义词**: gadget, instrument, tool
- **派生词**: devise (v.)

## devote
- **音标**: /dɪˈvoʊt/

- **释义**: 致力于；奉献
- **例句**: He devoted his entire career to medical research.
- **近义词**: dedicate, commit, give
- **派生词**: devotion (n.), devoted (adj.), devotee (n.)
