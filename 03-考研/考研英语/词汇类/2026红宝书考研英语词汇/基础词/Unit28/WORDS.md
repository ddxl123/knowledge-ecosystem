# Unit 28 单词

## subsequent
- **音标**: /ˈsʌbsɪkwənt/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 随后的；后来的
- **例句**: Subsequent events proved that he was right.
- **近义词**: following, later, ensuing
- **派生词**: subsequently (adv.)

## substance
- **音标**: /ˈsʌbstəns/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 物质；实质；根据
- **例句**: The substance of his argument was convincing.
- **近义词**: material, matter, essence
- **派生词**: substantial (adj.), substantiate (v.)

## substitute
- **音标**: /ˈsʌbstɪtuːt/
