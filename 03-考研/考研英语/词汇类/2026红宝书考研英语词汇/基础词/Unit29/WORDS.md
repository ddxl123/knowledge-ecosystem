## task
- **音标**: /tæsk/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 任务；工作
- **例句**: The task of rebuilding the city will take years.
- **近义词**: job, assignment, duty

## tax
- **音标**: /tæks/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 税；征税
- **例句**: The government raised taxes on luxury goods.
- **近义词**: levy, duty, tariff
- **派生词**: taxation (n.), taxpayer (n.), taxable (adj.)

## tear

- **派生词**: tearing (adj.), tearful (adj.)
