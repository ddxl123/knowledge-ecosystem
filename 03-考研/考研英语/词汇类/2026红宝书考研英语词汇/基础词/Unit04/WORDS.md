## caution
- **音标**: /ˈkɔːʃn/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 谨慎；小心；警告
- **例句**: You should proceed with caution in this matter.
- **近义词**: care, prudence, warning
- **派生词**: cautious (adj.), cautiously (adv.), precaution (n.)
