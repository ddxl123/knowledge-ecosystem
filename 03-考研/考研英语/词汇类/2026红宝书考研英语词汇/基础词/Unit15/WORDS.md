## insight
- **音标**: /ˈɪnsaɪt/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 洞察力；见解
- **例句**: The book provides valuable insights into human behavior.
- **近义词**: understanding, perception, wisdom
- **派生词**: insightful (adj.)

## insist
- **音标**: /ɪnˈsɪst/

- **近义词**: demand, persist, maintain
- **派生词**: insistence (n.), insistent (adj.), insistently (adv.)
