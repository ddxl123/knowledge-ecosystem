# 高频词汇 List04 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| launch a campaign | 发起一场运动 | They launched a campaign against corruption. |
| be capable of | 有能力做 | She is capable of great things. |
| capture one's attention | 吸引注意力 | The headline captured my attention. |
| with caution | 小心地 | Handle the equipment with caution. |
| cease to do/doing | 停止做 | The noise ceased to be a problem. |
| face a challenge | 面对挑战 | We all face challenges in life. |
| challenge sb. to do | 挑战某人做 | He challenged me to a race. |
| in charge of | 负责 | Who is in charge of this project? |
| charge sb. with | 指控某人 | He was charged with theft. |
| under the circumstances | 在这种情况下 | Under the circumstances, we had no choice. |
| under no circumstances | 决不 | Under no circumstances should you give up. |
| cite...as... | 引用…作为… | He cited the study as evidence. |
| make a claim | 提出声明 | She made a claim for compensation. |
| cling to | 坚持；紧握 | He clung to the hope of success. |
| combine...with... | 把…与…结合 | Combine the flour with the eggs. |
| collapse from | 因…而倒下 | He collapsed from exhaustion. |
| have a clue | 有线索 | I don't have a clue what's going on. |
| classify...into... | 把…分类为… | Books are classified into different categories. |
| in circulation | 在流通中 | The new currency is already in circulation. |
| chronic disease | 慢性疾病 | Diabetes is a chronic disease. |
