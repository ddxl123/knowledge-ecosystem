# 低频词汇 List10 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| impair one's ability | 损害某人的能力 | Lack of sleep impairs your ability to concentrate. |
| impart knowledge | 传授知识 | Good teachers impart knowledge effectively. |
| it is imperative that | …是必要的 | It is imperative that we take immediate action. |
| implicit in | 隐含在…中 | There is an implicit assumption in his argument. |
| have an inclination towards | 倾向于 | She has an inclination towards the arts. |
| incorporate...into | 把…纳入 | The new rules were incorporated into the policy. |
| indigenous to | 原产于 | This plant is indigenous to South America. |
| indispensable to/for | 对…不可或缺 | Sleep is indispensable for good health. |
| induce sb. to do | 诱导某人做 | The offer induced him to change his mind. |
| infrastructure development | 基础设施建设 | The government invested in infrastructure development. |
| inherent in | 固有于 | There are risks inherent in any business. |
| initiate a discussion | 发起讨论 | The teacher initiated a discussion on the topic. |
| innate talent | 天赋 | She has an innate talent for painting. |
| a man/woman of integrity | 正直的人 | He is a man of integrity and honesty. |
| intervene in | 干预 | The UN intervened in the conflict. |
| intricate details | 复杂的细节 | The painting is full of intricate details. |
| intrinsic value | 内在价值 | Gold has intrinsic value. |
| invoke a law | 援引法律 | The lawyer invoked a rarely used law. |
| an ironic twist | 讽刺的转折 | The story had an ironic twist at the end. |
| jeopardize the chances of | 危及…的机会 | His behavior jeopardized his chances of promotion. |
