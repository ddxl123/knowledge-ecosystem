# 低频词汇 List05 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| thrive on | 以…蓬勃发展 | She thrives on challenges. |
| tolerate doing | 容忍做 | I can't tolerate being treated like this. |
| trace back to | 追溯到 | The tradition traces back to the 18th century. |
| transfer...to | 把…转移到 | The money was transferred to his account. |
| transform...into | 把…转变为 | The old factory was transformed into a museum. |
| a growing trend | 日益增长的趋势 | There is a growing trend towards remote work. |
| trigger a response | 引发反应 | The announcement triggered a strong response. |
| triumph over | 战胜 | She triumphed over all difficulties. |
| undergo treatment | 接受治疗 | The patient is undergoing treatment for cancer. |
| undertake a task | 承担任务 | He undertook the task of organizing the event. |
| be unique to | 为…所独有 | This species is unique to Australia. |
| update sb. on | 向某人更新 | Please update me on the latest developments. |
| urge sb. to do | 催促某人做 | The doctor urged him to stop smoking. |
| be valid for | 有效期为 | The ticket is valid for three months. |
| vanish from | 从…消失 | The species has vanished from the wild. |
| vary from...to | 从…到…不等 | Temperatures vary from 10 to 30 degrees. |
| a vast number of | 大量的 | A vast number of people attended the event. |
| verify that | 核实 | Please verify that the information is correct. |
| a valid reason | 合理的理由 | He gave a valid reason for his absence. |
| under uniform standards | 在统一标准下 | Products are tested under uniform standards. |
