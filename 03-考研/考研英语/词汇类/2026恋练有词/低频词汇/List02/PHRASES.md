# 低频词汇 List02 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| in reward for | 作为对…的奖赏 | He received a medal in reward for his bravery. |
| get rid of | 摆脱 | We need to get rid of old habits. |
| play a role in | 在…中起作用 | Technology plays an important role in education. |
| on a large scale | 大规模地 | The project was carried out on a large scale. |
| ahead of schedule | 提前 | The project was completed ahead of schedule. |
| behind schedule | 落后于计划 | The construction is behind schedule. |
| on schedule | 按计划 | The train arrived on schedule. |
| within the scope of | 在…范围内 | The issue falls within the scope of this report. |
| be secure from | 免受…的安全 | The building is secure from theft. |
| seek to do | 试图做 | The company seeks to expand its market. |
| be sensitive to | 对…敏感 | She is sensitive to cold weather. |
| in sequence | 按顺序 | Please arrange the numbers in sequence. |
| severe weather | 恶劣天气 | Severe weather warnings have been issued. |
| a shift in | …的转变 | There has been a shift in consumer behavior. |
| shrink from | 退缩于 | He didn't shrink from his responsibilities. |
| be similar to | 与…相似 | His idea is similar to mine. |
| simplify one's life | 简化生活 | She decided to simplify her life. |
| a significant role | 重要作用 | Education plays a significant role in development. |
| sacrifice...for | 为…牺牲 | She sacrificed her career for her children. |
| select...from | 从…中选择 | The committee selected the winner from many candidates. |
