# 低频词汇 List01 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| rely on/upon | 依赖 | You can always rely on her for support. |
| be reluctant to do | 不情愿做 | He was reluctant to admit his mistake. |
| remain silent | 保持沉默 | She remained silent throughout the meeting. |
| beyond remedy | 无可救药 | The damage was beyond remedy. |
| render...+adj. | 使…成为…状态 | The shock rendered him speechless. |
| renew one's subscription | 续订 | I renewed my subscription to the magazine. |
| replace...with | 用…替换 | We replaced the old system with a new one. |
| be represented by | 由…代表 | The company is represented by a lawyer. |
| have a reputation for | 以…闻名 | The restaurant has a reputation for good food. |
| on request | 一经要求 | Details are available on request. |
| require sb. to do | 要求某人做 | The law requires employers to provide insurance. |
| resemble...in | 在…方面像 | She resembles her sister in appearance. |
| reserve the right to | 保留做…的权利 | We reserve the right to refuse service. |
| resign from | 从…辞职 | She resigned from the board. |
| resist the temptation | 抵制诱惑 | He couldn't resist the temptation to eat the cake. |
| resolve to do | 下决心做 | He resolved to change his lifestyle. |
| respond to | 对…作出回应 | The company responded to the criticism quickly. |
| restore...to | 把…恢复到 | The painting was restored to its original condition. |
| be restricted to | 被限制在 | Access is restricted to authorized personnel. |
| retain one's title | 保住头衔 | The champion retained her title for the third year. |
