# 中频词汇 List07 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| objective evidence | 客观证据 | We need objective evidence to support the claim. |
| be obliged to do | 有义务做 | Citizens are obliged to pay taxes. |
| observe the rules | 遵守规则 | All employees must observe the rules. |
| obtain a degree | 获得学位 | She obtained a master's degree in economics. |
| be occupied with | 忙于 | He is occupied with his research. |
| It occurs to sb. that | 某人突然想到 | It occurred to me that I had forgotten my keys. |
| take offense at | 对…生气 | He took offense at her remark. |
| oppose doing | 反对做 | She opposed building a highway through the park. |
| have no option but to | 别无选择只能 | We had no option but to agree. |
| in the original | 用原文 | The book is better in the original. |
| originate from/in | 起源于 | The custom originates from ancient times. |
| the outcome of | …的结果 | The outcome of the negotiations was positive. |
| outline a plan | 概述计划 | The manager outlined the plan for the next quarter. |
| overcome difficulties | 克服困难 | She overcame many difficulties in her life. |
| be overwhelmed by | 被…压倒 | She was overwhelmed by the amount of work. |
| owing to | 由于 | Owing to the rain, the match was postponed. |
| keep pace with | 跟上…的步伐 | Technology is hard to keep pace with. |
| participate in | 参加 | All members are expected to participate in the event. |
| in particular | 尤其 | I enjoy all music, classical in particular. |
| have a passion for | 对…充满热情 | He has a passion for cooking. |
