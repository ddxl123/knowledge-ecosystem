# 中频词汇 List10 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| qualify for | 有资格获得 | She qualifies for the scholarship. |
| at random | 随机地 | The winners were selected at random. |
| a wide range of | 广泛的 | The university offers a wide range of courses. |
| rank first | 排名第一 | The university ranks first in the country. |
| at a rapid pace | 以快速的步伐 | Technology is advancing at a rapid pace. |
| rate...as | 把…评为 | The film was rated as one of the best. |
| react to | 对…作出反应 | How did the market react to the news? |
| in reality | 实际上 | In reality, the situation is more complex. |
| recall doing | 回忆做 | I don't recall meeting him before. |
| be recognized as | 被认为是 | He is recognized as an expert in the field. |
| recommend doing | 建议做 | I recommend reading this book. |
| recover from | 从…恢复 | She recovered from the shock quickly. |
| reduce...to | 把…减少到 | Costs were reduced to a minimum. |
| refer to | 提到；参考 | Please refer to the instructions. |
| reflect on | 反思 | Take time to reflect on your experience. |
| carry out reforms | 进行改革 | The government carried out major reforms. |
| register for | 注册 | You need to register for the exam. |
| be related to | 与…有关 | The disease is related to poor diet. |
| be relevant to | 与…相关 | This information is relevant to the case. |
| release...from | 从…释放 | He was released from prison last month. |
