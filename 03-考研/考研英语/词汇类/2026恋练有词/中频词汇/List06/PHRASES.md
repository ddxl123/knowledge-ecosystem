# 中频词汇 List06 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be manufactured in | 在…制造 | The product is manufactured in China. |
| by a narrow margin | 以微弱优势 | He won the election by a narrow margin. |
| a mass of | 大量的 | A mass of people gathered in the square. |
| to the maximum | 最大限度地 | We should utilize resources to the maximum. |
| mental health | 心理健康 | Mental health awareness is increasing. |
| on one's merits | 按其优点 | Candidates are judged on their merits. |
| migrate from...to | 从…迁移到 | Workers migrated from rural areas to cities. |
| minimum wage | 最低工资 | The government raised the minimum wage. |
| a minor role | 次要角色 | He played a minor role in the movie. |
| moderate views | 温和的观点 | She holds moderate views on politics. |
| modify one's behavior | 改变行为 | Education can help modify people's behavior. |
| monitor the situation | 监控形势 | The government is monitoring the situation closely. |
| in the mood for | 有心情做 | I'm not in the mood for going out. |
| moral dilemma | 道德困境 | The issue presents a moral dilemma. |
| motivate sb. to do | 激励某人做 | The coach motivated the team to win. |
| by mutual consent | 经双方同意 | The contract was terminated by mutual consent. |
| narrow down | 缩小范围 | We need to narrow down the options. |
| neglect one's duty | 玩忽职守 | He was accused of neglecting his duty. |
| negotiate with | 与…谈判 | The union negotiated with the management. |
| nevertheless | 然而 | It was difficult; nevertheless, they succeeded. |
