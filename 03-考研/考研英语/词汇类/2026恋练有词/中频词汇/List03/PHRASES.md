# 中频词汇 List03 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| guarantee sth. to sb. | 向某人保证某事 | The company guarantees quality to its customers. |
| guide sb. through | 指导某人完成 | The mentor guided her through the process. |
| be guilty of | 犯有…罪 | He was guilty of theft. |
| handle with care | 小心轻放 | This package should be handled with care. |
| harsh reality | 严酷的现实 | We must face the harsh reality. |
| highlight the importance of | 强调…的重要性 | The study highlights the importance of sleep. |
| on the horizon | 在地平线上；即将来临 | A new opportunity is on the horizon. |
| be hostile to | 对…有敌意 | The crowd was hostile to the speaker. |
| be identical to/with | 与…完全相同 | His opinion is identical to mine. |
| identify...as | 把…确认为 | The witness identified him as the thief. |
| be ignorant of | 不了解 | She was ignorant of the facts. |
| illustrate...with | 用…来说明 | He illustrated his point with a story. |
| have an impact on | 对…有影响 | Technology has a great impact on our lives. |
| imply that | 暗示 | Are you implying that I'm wrong? |
| impose...on | 把…强加于 | Don't impose your views on others. |
| be impressed by/with | 对…印象深刻 | We were impressed by his performance. |
| on impulse | 一时冲动 | She quit her job on impulse. |
| be inclined to | 倾向于 | I'm inclined to believe him. |
| indicate that | 表明 | The results indicate that the treatment works. |
| individual effort | 个人努力 | Success requires individual effort. |
