# 中频词汇 List02 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be flexible about | 对…灵活 | We should be flexible about the schedule. |
| focus on | 集中于 | Let's focus on the key issues. |
| forbid sb. from doing | 禁止某人做 | The rules forbid employees from using phones. |
| forecast...to be | 预测…为 | Sales are forecast to be higher this year. |
| make a fortune | 发财 | He made a fortune selling real estate. |
| a fraction of | 一小部分 | Only a fraction of applicants are accepted. |
| be frustrated at/by | 对…感到沮丧 | She was frustrated by the lack of progress. |
| fulfill a promise | 履行承诺 | He finally fulfilled his promise. |
| fulfill a role | 起到…作用 | Technology fulfills an important role in education. |
| gain access to | 获得…的使用权 | Students gain access to online resources. |
| gain experience | 获得经验 | Internships help you gain experience. |
| bridge the gap | 弥补差距 | We need to bridge the gap between theory and practice. |
| gather information | 收集信息 | The police are gathering information about the case. |
| generate income | 产生收入 | The project generates income for the community. |
| be governed by | 受…支配 | Our actions are governed by laws. |
| take...for granted | 认为…理所当然 | Don't take your health for granted. |
| grant sb. sth. | 授予某人某物 | The university granted her a degree. |
| grasp the meaning | 理解含义 | It's hard to grasp the meaning of this poem. |
| global warming | 全球变暖 | Global warming affects everyone. |
| beyond one's grasp | 超出理解范围 | The theory was beyond my grasp. |
