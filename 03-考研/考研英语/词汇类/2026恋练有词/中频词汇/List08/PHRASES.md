# 中频词汇 List08 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be perceived as | 被认为是 | He is perceived as a strong leader. |
| permanent residence | 永久居留 | She obtained permanent residence in Canada. |
| permit sb. to do | 允许某人做 | The teacher permitted students to leave early. |
| persist in doing | 坚持做 | She persisted in working despite the pain. |
| from one's perspective | 从某人的角度来看 | From my perspective, the plan is feasible. |
| social phenomenon | 社会现象 | Unemployment is a social phenomenon. |
| pose a threat | 构成威胁 | Climate change poses a threat to the planet. |
| be positive about | 对…持乐观态度 | She is positive about the future. |
| potential risk | 潜在风险 | We should consider the potential risks. |
| practical experience | 实践经验 | Practical experience is as important as theory. |
| be preceded by | 在…之前 | The lecture was preceded by a brief introduction. |
| be predicted to | 被预测为 | The economy is predicted to grow by 3%. |
| prejudice against | 对…的偏见 | There is prejudice against immigrants. |
| preliminary study | 初步研究 | A preliminary study was conducted. |
| on the premise that | 以…为前提 | The plan was made on the premise that funding would be available. |
| preserve...from | 保护…免受 | Sunscreen helps preserve skin from damage. |
| under pressure | 在压力下 | He works well under pressure. |
| practical application | 实际应用 | The theory has many practical applications. |
| in poverty | 处于贫困中 | Many families still live in poverty. |
| predominant role | 主导作用 | Education plays a predominant role in development. |
