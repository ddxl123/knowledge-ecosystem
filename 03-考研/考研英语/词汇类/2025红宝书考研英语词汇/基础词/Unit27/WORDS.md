## spontaneous
- **音标**: /spɑːnˈteɪniəs/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 自发的；自然的
- **例句**: The crowd broke into spontaneous applause.
- **近义词**: impulsive, unplanned, natural
- **派生词**: spontaneously (adv.), spontaneity (n.)

- **释义**: 地点；斑点；发现

- **近义词**: location, place, notice
- **派生词**: spotlight (n.), spotted (adj.), spotless (adj.)
