# Unit 01 单词

## absolute
- **音标**: /ˈæbsəluːt/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 绝对的；完全的
- **例句**: You have my absolute support.
- **近义词**: complete, total, utter
- **派生词**: absolutely (adv.), absolutism (n.)

## absorb
- **音标**: /əbˈzɔːrb/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 吸收；吸引；使全神贯注
- **例句**: Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the air.
- **近义词**: soak up, take in, consume
- **派生词**: absorption (n.), absorbed (adj.), absorbing (adj.)

## abstract
- **音标**: /ˈæbstrækt/
