## predict
- **音标**: /prɪˈdɪkt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 预测；预言
- **例句**: It's difficult to predict the outcome of the election.
- **近义词**: forecast, foresee, anticipate
- **派生词**: prediction (n.), predictable (adj.), unpredictable (adj.)

## predominant
- **音标**: /prɪˈdɑːmɪnənt/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 占主导地位的；显著的
- **例句**: English is the predominant language in international business.
- **近义词**: dominant, chief, main
- **派生词**: predominantly (adv.), predominate (v.)

## prefer
