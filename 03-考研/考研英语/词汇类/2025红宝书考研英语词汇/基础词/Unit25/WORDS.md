## sample
- **音标**: /ˈsæmpl/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 样品；样本；品尝
- **例句**: The company distributed free samples of the new product.
- **近义词**: specimen, example, try
- **派生词**: sampling (n.)

## satellite
- **音标**: /ˈsætəlaɪt/

- **释义**: 卫星；人造卫星
- **例句**: The satellite orbits the Earth every 24 hours.
- **近义词**: moon, spacecraft
