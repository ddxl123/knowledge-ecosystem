## authority
- **音标**: /əˈθɔːrəti/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 权威；当局；权力
- **例句**: The local authority is responsible for public services.
- **近义词**: power, control, jurisdiction
- **派生词**: authoritative (adj.), authorize (v.), authorization (n.)

## automatic
- **音标**: /ˌɔːtəˈmætɪk/
- **词性**: adj. n.
- **释义**: 自动的；无意识的
- **例句**: The door has an automatic locking system.
- **近义词**: self-operating, mechanical
- **派生词**: automatically (adv.), automation (n.), automate (v.)

## available
- **音标**: /əˈveɪləbl/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 可用的；有空的
- **例句**: The book is available in all major bookstores.
- **近义词**: accessible, obtainable, free
- **派生词**: availability (n.), unavailable (adj.)

## average
- **音标**: /ˈævərɪdʒ/
- **词性**: n. adj. v.
- **释义**: 平均；平均的
- **例句**: The average temperature in July is 30°C.
- **近义词**: mean, typical, normal
- **派生词**: averaging (n.)
