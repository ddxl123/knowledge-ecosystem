## mature
- **音标**: /məˈtʃʊr/
- **词性**: adj. v.
- **释义**: 成熟的；使成熟
- **例句**: He is mature enough to make his own decisions.
- **近义词**: adult, ripe, developed
- **派生词**: maturity (n.), immature (adj.)

## maximum
- **音标**: /ˈmæksɪməm/
- **词性**: n. adj.
- **释义**: 最大值；最大的
- **例句**: The maximum speed limit is 120 km/h.
- **近义词**: peak, highest, utmost
- **派生词**: maximize (v.), maximal (adj.)
