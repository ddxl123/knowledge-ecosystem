## decade
- **音标**: /ˈdekeɪd/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 十年
- **例句**: The city has changed a lot over the past decade.
- **近义词**: ten years

## decline
- **音标**: /dɪˈklaɪn/
- **词性**: v. n.
- **释义**: 下降；拒绝；衰退
- **例句**: Sales have declined by 10% this year.
- **近义词**: decrease, refuse, diminish
- **派生词**: declining (adj.)

## decorate
