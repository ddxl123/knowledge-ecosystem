## optimistic
- **音标**: /ˌɑːptɪˈmɪstɪk/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 乐观的
- **例句**: She is optimistic about the future of the company.
- **近义词**: hopeful, positive, upbeat
- **派生词**: optimism (n.), optimist (n.), pessimistic (反义)

## option
- **音标**: /ˈɑːpʃn/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 选择；选项
- **例句**: We have several options to choose from.
- **近义词**: choice, alternative, selection
- **派生词**: optional (adj.), optionally (adv.)

## orbit

- **派生词**: orbital (adj.)
