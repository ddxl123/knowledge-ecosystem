## cultivate
- **音标**: /ˈkʌltɪveɪt/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 培养；耕作；栽培
- **例句**: Schools should cultivate students' creativity.
- **近义词**: develop, nurture, foster
- **派生词**: cultivation (n.), cultivated (adj.), cultivator (n.)
