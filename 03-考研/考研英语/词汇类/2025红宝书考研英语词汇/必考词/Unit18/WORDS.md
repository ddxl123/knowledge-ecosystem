# Unit 18 单词

## pause
- **音标**: /pɔːz/
- **词性**: v. n.
- **释义**: 暂停；中断
- **例句**: She paused for a moment before answering the question.
- **近义词**: stop, halt, break
- **派生词**: pausing (adj.)

## peak
- **音标**: /piːk/
- **词性**: n. adj. v.
- **释义**: 顶峰；高峰的；达到顶点
- **例句**: The company reached its peak in 2010.
- **近义词**: summit, top, maximum
- **派生词**: peaked (adj.)

## peculiar
- **音标**: /pɪˈkjuːliər/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 奇怪的；特有的
- **例句**: There's a peculiar smell in this room.
- **近义词**: strange, odd, distinctive
- **派生词**: peculiarity (n.), peculiarly (adv.)

## peer

- **派生词**: peerless (adj.)
