# Unit 13 单词

## harvest
- **音标**: /ˈhɑːrvɪst/
- **词性**: n. v.
- **释义**: 收获；收割；成果
- **例句**: The farmers celebrated a good harvest this year.
- **近义词**: crop, yield, reap
- **派生词**: harvester (n.)

## haste
- **音标**: /heɪst/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 匆忙；急忙
- **例句**: In his haste to leave, he forgot his keys.
- **近义词**: hurry, rush, speed
- **派生词**: hasten (v.), hasty (adj.), hastily (adv.)
