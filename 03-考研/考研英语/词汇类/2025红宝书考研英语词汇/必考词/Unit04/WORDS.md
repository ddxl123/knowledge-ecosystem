## cancel
- **音标**: /ˈkænsəl/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 取消；撤销；删去
- **例句**: The flight was cancelled due to bad weather.
- **近义词**: call off, revoke, annul
- **派生词**: cancellation (n.)
