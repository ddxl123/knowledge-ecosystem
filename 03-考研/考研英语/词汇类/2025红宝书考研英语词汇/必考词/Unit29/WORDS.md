## conscience
- **音标**: /ˈkɑːnʃəns/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 良心；道德心
- **例句**: He has a guilty conscience about lying to his friend.
- **近义词**: morality, principles, scruples
- **派生词**: conscientious (adj.), conscientiously (adv.)

## consensus
- **音标**: /kənˈsensəs/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 共识；一致
- **例句**: The committee reached a consensus on the issue.
- **近义词**: agreement, accord, harmony
- **派生词**: consensual (adj.)

## conserve

- **近义词**: preserve, save, protect
- **派生词**: conservation (n.), conservative (adj. n.)
