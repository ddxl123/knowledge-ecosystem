## fortune
- **音标**: /ˈfɔːrtʃən/
- **词性**: n.
- **释义**: 命运；财富；运气
- **例句**: He made a fortune in the stock market.
- **近义词**: wealth, luck, destiny
- **派生词**: fortunate (adj.), fortunately (adv.), unfortunate (adj.)

## foster
- **音标**: /ˈfɔːstər/

- **释义**: 培养；促进；收养的
- **例句**: Schools should foster creativity in students.
- **近义词**: encourage, promote, nurture
- **派生词**: fosterage (n.)
