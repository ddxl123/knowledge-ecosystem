## mild
- **音标**: /maɪld/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 温和的；轻微的；淡味的
- **例句**: The weather is mild today, not too hot or cold.
- **近义词**: gentle, moderate, soft
- **派生词**: mildly (adv.), mildness (n.)

## military
- **音标**: /ˈmɪlɪteri/
- **词性**: adj. n.
- **释义**: 军事的；军队
- **例句**: He served in the military for ten years.
- **近义词**: armed forces, army, martial
- **派生词**: militarize (v.), militarily (adv.)

## minimize
