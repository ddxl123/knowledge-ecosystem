## acknowledge
- **音标**: /əkˈnɑːlɪdʒ/
- **词性**: v.
- **释义**: 承认；致谢；确认收到
- **例句**: He acknowledged his mistake and apologized.
- **近义词**: admit, recognize, concede
- **派生词**: acknowledgment (n.)

## adequate
- **音标**: /ˈædɪkwət/
- **词性**: adj.
- **释义**: 足够的；适当的
- **例句**: The supply of food is adequate for the refugees.
- **近义词**: sufficient, enough, satisfactory
- **派生词**: adequacy (n.), inadequacy (n.)

## advocate

- **派生词**: advocacy (n.)
