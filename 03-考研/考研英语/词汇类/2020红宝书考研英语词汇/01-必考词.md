# 必考词

## 核心考点

- **高频词汇:** 考研英语大纲5500词中最常考的约2000词
- **熟词僻义:** 常见单词的不常见含义（如 address=处理，figure=认为）
- **一词多义:** 同一单词在不同语境中的含义差异
- **词组搭配:** 动词+介词、形容词+名词的固定搭配

## 高频必考词分类

### 动词高频词
- abandon, abstract, accommodate, accumulate, acknowledge
- advocate, allocate, anticipate, appreciate, assume
- claim, clarify, collapse, commit, comply, conceive
- decline, demonstrate, derive, detect, diminish

### 名词高频词
- achievement, administration, affection, alternative
- approach, authority, awareness, barrier, benefit
- campaign, capacity, circumstance, committee, community

### 形容词高频词
- abundant, accessible, adequate, aggressive, apparent
- artificial, authentic, beneficial, comprehensive, considerable

## 记忆方法

- 词根词缀法：-spect（看）→ inspect, prospect, retrospect, spectacle
- 语境记忆：在真题阅读中记忆单词
- 词频记忆：按出现频率排序，优先攻克高频词
- 联想记忆：建立单词之间的关联网络
