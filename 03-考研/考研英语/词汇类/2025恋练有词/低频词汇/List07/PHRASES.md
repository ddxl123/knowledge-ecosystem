# 低频词汇 List07 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be compatible with | 与…兼容 | This software is compatible with Windows 10. |
| comprise of | 由…组成 | The team comprises of experts from various fields. |
| conform to/with | 遵守 | Products must conform to safety standards. |
| for consecutive days | 连续几天 | It rained for five consecutive days. |
| give consent to | 同意 | The parents gave consent to the surgery. |
| consolidate one's position | 巩固地位 | The company consolidated its position in the market. |
| budget constraint | 预算限制 | Budget constraints forced us to cut costs. |
| contemplate doing | 考虑做 | She contemplated moving abroad. |
| show contempt for | 对…表示蔑视 | He showed contempt for the law. |
| be contrary to | 与…相反 | His actions are contrary to his words. |
| cause controversy | 引起争议 | The decision caused a lot of controversy. |
| convene a meeting | 召开会议 | The board convened a meeting to discuss the issue. |
| conventional methods | 传统方法 | Conventional methods are no longer effective. |
| the cornerstone of | …的基石 | Trust is the cornerstone of any relationship. |
| a deficit in | 在…方面的不足 | There is a deficit in funding for education. |
| defy description | 难以形容 | The beauty of the place defies description. |
| demographic change | 人口变化 | Demographic change affects the labor market. |
| depict...as | 把…描绘为 | The media depicted him as a hero. |
| deplete of | 耗尽 | The soil was depleted of nutrients. |
| deteriorate into | 恶化为 | The situation deteriorated into chaos. |
