# 低频词汇 List06 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| fall victim to | 成为…的受害者 | Many people fall victim to online scams. |
| violate the law | 违法 | Anyone who violates the law will be punished. |
| virtual reality | 虚拟现实 | Virtual reality technology is improving rapidly. |
| be visible to | 对…可见 | The building is visible from miles away. |
| be vital to/for | 对…至关重要 | Sleep is vital to good health. |
| a vivid memory | 清晰的记忆 | She has a vivid memory of the event. |
| on a voluntary basis | 自愿地 | The program operates on a voluntary basis. |
| be vulnerable to | 易受…伤害 | Coastal areas are vulnerable to flooding. |
| raise wages | 提高工资 | The union demanded to raise wages. |
| social welfare | 社会福利 | Social welfare programs help the poor. |
| widespread belief | 普遍的信念 | There is a widespread belief that education is the key to success. |
| withdraw from | 从…撤回 | He decided to withdraw from the competition. |
| witness to | …的目击者 | She was a witness to the crime. |
| yield to | 屈服于 | He refused to yield to pressure. |
| a worthwhile effort | 值得的努力 | Helping others is always a worthwhile effort. |
| whereas | 然而 | He is tall, whereas his brother is short. |
| in the zone | 进入状态 | The athlete was in the zone during the competition. |
| academic achievement | 学术成就 | Academic achievement is important for career prospects. |
| annual report | 年度报告 | The company published its annual report. |
| anticipate doing | 预期做 | We didn't anticipate having so many visitors. |
