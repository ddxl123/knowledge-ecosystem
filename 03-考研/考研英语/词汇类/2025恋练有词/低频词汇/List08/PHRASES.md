# 低频词汇 List08 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be displaced by | 被…取代 | Many workers were displaced by automation. |
| disrupt the peace | 扰乱和平 | The protest disrupted the peace in the city. |
| divert...from | 把…从…转移 | The funds were diverted from education to defense. |
| suffer from drought | 遭受干旱 | The region suffered from severe drought. |
| be eligible for | 有资格获得 | She is eligible for retirement benefits. |
| be embedded in | 根植于 | The tradition is embedded in the culture. |
| embody the spirit of | 体现…的精神 | The athlete embodies the spirit of perseverance. |
| empirical evidence | 实证证据 | The theory is supported by empirical evidence. |
| endorse a candidate | 支持候选人 | The party endorsed the candidate for president. |
| enhance one's ability | 提高能力 | Practice enhances your ability to speak English. |
| enlighten sb. on | 启发某人关于 | The documentary enlightened viewers on the issue. |
| envision a future | 展望未来 | She envisioned a future of peace and prosperity. |
| an epidemic of | …的流行 | There is an epidemic of obesity in many countries. |
| reach equilibrium | 达到平衡 | The economy eventually reached equilibrium. |
| erode confidence | 削弱信心 | The scandal eroded public confidence in the government. |
| in essence | 本质上 | In essence, the two proposals are the same. |
| ethnic minority | 少数民族 | The rights of ethnic minorities must be protected. |
| evacuate from | 从…疏散 | People were evacuated from the flooded area. |
| exaggerate the importance of | 夸大…的重要性 | He tends to exaggerate the importance of his role. |
| excavate a site | 挖掘遗址 | The team spent years excavating the archaeological site. |
