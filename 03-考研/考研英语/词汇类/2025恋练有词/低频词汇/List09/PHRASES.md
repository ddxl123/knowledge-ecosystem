# 低频词汇 List09 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| an excerpt from | 摘自 | She read an excerpt from the book. |
| be exempt from | 免除 | Senior citizens are exempt from the charge. |
| expertise in | 在…方面的专业知识 | She has expertise in artificial intelligence. |
| be explicit about | 对…明确 | The rules are explicit about what is allowed. |
| extract...from | 从…中提取 | They extracted the juice from the fruit. |
| fabricate a story | 编造故事 | He fabricated a story to avoid punishment. |
| be feasible for | 对…可行 | The plan is feasible for a small company. |
| a flaw in | …中的缺陷 | There is a flaw in the argument. |
| foster a sense of | 培养…感 | Schools should foster a sense of community. |
| express gratitude for | 对…表示感激 | She expressed gratitude for their support. |
| the gravity of | …的严重性 | The gravity of the situation cannot be ignored. |
| natural habitat | 自然栖息地 | We must protect the natural habitat of endangered species. |
| hamper the growth of | 阻碍…的发展 | Corruption hampers the growth of the economy. |
| a health hazard | 健康隐患 | Air pollution is a serious health hazard. |
| at the top of the hierarchy | 在等级制度的顶端 | The CEO is at the top of the hierarchy. |
| test a hypothesis | 检验假设 | The experiment was designed to test the hypothesis. |
| be immersed in | 沉浸于 | She was immersed in her research. |
| be immune to | 对…免疫 | Some people are immune to the disease. |
| ideological differences | 意识形态差异 | The two parties have ideological differences. |
| a sense of hierarchy | 等级观念 | A strong sense of hierarchy exists in the organization. |
