# 低频词汇 List03 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be skeptical about | 对…怀疑 | Many people are skeptical about the claim. |
| a slight difference | 微小的差别 | There is only a slight difference between the two. |
| slip one's mind | 被遗忘 | His name slipped my mind. |
| sole purpose | 唯一目的 | The sole purpose of the meeting was to vote. |
| source of income | 收入来源 | Tourism is the main source of income for the island. |
| be specific about | 对…具体 | Please be specific about your requirements. |
| speculate on/about | 推测 | People speculated about the cause of the accident. |
| at stake | 处于危险中 | Millions of jobs are at stake. |
| set a standard | 设定标准 | The school sets a high standard for its students. |
| state of mind | 心态 | A positive state of mind is important for success. |
| steady growth | 稳步增长 | The economy has experienced steady growth. |
| economic stimulus | 经济刺激 | The government announced an economic stimulus package. |
| lay/put stress on | 强调 | The teacher laid stress on the importance of practice. |
| a striking feature | 引人注目的特征 | The most striking feature of the building is its height. |
| be strict with | 对…严格 | Parents should be strict with their children. |
| stress the importance of | 强调…的重要性 | The report stresses the importance of education. |
| a stretch of | 一片 | There is a beautiful stretch of beach nearby. |
| strengthen ties | 加强联系 | The two countries agreed to strengthen ties. |
| be structured around | 围绕…构建 | The course is structured around weekly lectures. |
| status quo | 现状 | They want to maintain the status quo. |
