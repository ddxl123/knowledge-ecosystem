# 中频词汇 List09 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| prevail over/against | 战胜 | Good always prevails over evil in the end. |
| prevent...from doing | 阻止…做 | The rain prevented us from going out. |
| prior to | 在…之前 | Prior to the meeting, please submit your report. |
| give priority to | 优先考虑 | We should give priority to education. |
| the privilege of doing | 做…的荣幸 | I had the privilege of meeting the president. |
| proceed with | 继续进行 | Let's proceed with the discussion. |
| be in the process of | 正在…过程中 | We are in the process of reviewing the applications. |
| profit from | 从…获利 | She profited from the experience. |
| make progress | 取得进步 | The student has made great progress. |
| prohibit...from doing | 禁止…做 | The law prohibits children from buying alcohol. |
| promote...to | 提升…到 | He was promoted to manager. |
| in proportion to | 与…成比例 | Payment is in proportion to the amount of work done. |
| propose a toast | 提议举杯 | He proposed a toast to the host. |
| pursue one's dream | 追求梦想 | She pursued her dream of becoming a doctor. |
| prove to be | 证明是 | The method proved to be effective. |
| provide...with | 提供 | The school provides students with textbooks. |
| provoke sb. into doing | 激怒某人做 | His words provoked her into responding angrily. |
| beyond prospect | 没有希望 | A quick solution is beyond prospect. |
| in protest | 抗议 | The workers went on strike in protest. |
| a profound impact | 深远的影响 | The event had a profound impact on society. |
