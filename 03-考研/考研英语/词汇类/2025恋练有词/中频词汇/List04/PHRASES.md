# 中频词汇 List04 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| influence on | 对…的影响 | Teachers have a great influence on students. |
| be informed of | 被告知 | You will be informed of the decision. |
| for instance | 例如 | Many countries, for instance, Japan, have aging populations. |
| insist on/upon | 坚持 | She insisted on her innocence. |
| inspire sb. to do | 激励某人做 | Her speech inspired me to take action. |
| install...in | 在…安装 | They installed cameras in the building. |
| the initial stage | 初期阶段 | The project is in the initial stage. |
| give insight into | 提供对…的洞察 | The study gives insight into consumer behavior. |
| integrate...into | 把…融入 | Technology is integrated into modern education. |
| intend to do | 打算做 | I intend to apply for the position. |
| interact with | 与…互动 | Students interact with each other in groups. |
| interpret...as | 把…解释为 | His silence was interpreted as agreement. |
| intervene in | 干预 | The UN intervened in the conflict. |
| investigate into | 调查 | The committee is investigating into the matter. |
| intellectual property | 知识产权 | Intellectual property should be protected. |
| an instrument for | …的工具 | Language is an instrument for communication. |
| insurance against | 对…的保险 | Travel insurance provides insurance against loss. |
| be intended for | 专为…设计 | This book is intended for beginners. |
| intense competition | 激烈的竞争 | The market is characterized by intense competition. |
| at the instance of | 应…的要求 | The meeting was called at the instance of the chairman. |
