# 中频词汇 List05 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| invest in | 投资于 | She invested in real estate. |
| be involved in | 参与；卷入 | He was involved in the project from the start. |
| be isolated from | 与…隔绝 | The village is isolated from the outside world. |
| raise an issue | 提出问题 | She raised an important issue at the meeting. |
| be keen on | 热衷于 | She is keen on playing tennis. |
| label...as | 把…称为 | He was labeled as a troublemaker. |
| launch a product | 发布产品 | Apple launched a new iPhone last month. |
| lean on | 依靠 | She leaned on her friends for support. |
| be linked to | 与…有关 | Obesity is linked to poor diet. |
| literary works | 文学作品 | Shakespeare's literary works are timeless. |
| be loaded with | 装满 | The truck was loaded with supplies. |
| lobby for | 为…游说 | Environmentalists lobbied for stricter laws. |
| be located in | 位于 | The school is located in the city center. |
| maintain that | 坚持认为 | He maintained that he was innocent. |
| manage to do | 设法做成 | She managed to finish the project on time. |
| in a manner | 以…方式 | He handled the situation in a professional manner. |
| by all manners of means | 尽一切办法 | We must succeed by all manners of means. |
| the majority of | 大多数 | The majority of students passed the exam. |
| a load of | 大量的 | She has a load of work to do. |
| in leisure time | 在闲暇时间 | He reads books in his leisure time. |
