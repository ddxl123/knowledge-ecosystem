# 高频词汇 List01 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| abandon oneself to | 沉溺于 | Don't abandon yourself to despair. |
| be absorbed in | 全神贯注于 | She was absorbed in her reading. |
| have access to | 有权使用/接触 | All citizens should have access to healthcare. |
| account for | 解释；占（比例） | How do you account for the delay? |
| accuse sb. of sth. | 指控某人某事 | He was accused of fraud. |
| achieve one's goal | 实现目标 | She finally achieved her goal. |
| adapt to | 适应 | It takes time to adapt to a new culture. |
| adhere to | 坚持；遵守 | We must adhere to the schedule. |
| adjust to | 适应 | He quickly adjusted to the new job. |
| advocate for | 提倡；主张 | They advocate for environmental protection. |
| have an advantage over | 比…有优势 | Experience gives her an advantage over others. |
| in advance | 预先；提前 | Please book your tickets in advance. |
| take advantage of | 利用 | We should take advantage of this opportunity. |
| be adequate for | 足够…用 | The funds are adequate for the project. |
| a great deal of | 大量的 | He has accumulated a great deal of experience. |
| be accused of | 被指控 | The official was accused of corruption. |
| acquire a taste for | 开始喜欢 | She acquired a taste for classical music. |
| in accordance with | 依照；根据 | In accordance with the law, the company was fined. |
| on account of | 因为；由于 | The game was cancelled on account of rain. |
| by all accounts | 据大家所说 | By all accounts, it was a wonderful event. |
