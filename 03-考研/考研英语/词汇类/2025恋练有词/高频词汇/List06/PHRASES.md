# 高频词汇 List06 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| be confused about | 对…感到困惑 | I'm confused about the new rules. |
| reach a consensus | 达成共识 | The team reached a consensus on the plan. |
| by consent of | 经…同意 | The meeting was held by consent of all members. |
| as a consequence of | 由于…的结果 | As a consequence of the war, many people lost their homes. |
| in consequence | 因此 | He didn't study, and in consequence, he failed. |
| conservation of energy | 能量守恒 | The conservation of energy is a fundamental law. |
| all things considered | 考虑到所有情况 | All things considered, it was a successful event. |
| be consistent with | 与…一致 | His actions are consistent with his words. |
| be conscious of | 意识到 | She was conscious of being watched. |
| constitute a threat | 构成威胁 | Pollution constitutes a threat to public health. |
| under construction | 在建设中 | The new building is under construction. |
| consult with | 与…商量 | You should consult with your advisor. |
| be content with | 对…满足 | He is content with his current job. |
| in the context of | 在…的背景下 | We must consider this in the context of history. |
| on the contrary | 恰恰相反 | He is not lazy; on the contrary, he works very hard. |
| contribute to | 有助于；导致 | Exercise contributes to good health. |
| a controversial issue | 有争议的问题 | Abortion remains a controversial issue. |
| convince sb. of sth. | 使某人确信某事 | He convinced me of his sincerity. |
| cope with | 应对 | How do you cope with pressure? |
| correspond to/with | 与…对应/一致 | The results correspond to our expectations. |
