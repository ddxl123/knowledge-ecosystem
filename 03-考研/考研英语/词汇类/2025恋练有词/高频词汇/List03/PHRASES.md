# 高频词汇 List03 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| attach importance to | 重视 | We should attach importance to education. |
| be attached to | 附属于；依恋 | She is deeply attached to her hometown. |
| attribute...to... | 把…归因于 | He attributed the failure to bad luck. |
| on behalf of | 代表 | I accept the award on behalf of the team. |
| benefit from | 从…受益 | Students benefit from reading widely. |
| be aware of | 意识到 | He was not aware of the danger. |
| ban sb. from doing sth. | 禁止某人做某事 | He was banned from driving for six months. |
| be based on | 以…为基础 | The movie is based on a true story. |
| bear in mind | 牢记 | Bear in mind that time is limited. |
| be to blame | 应受责备 | Who is to blame for the accident? |
| block...from doing | 阻止…做 | The law blocks companies from exploiting workers. |
| be bound to | 一定会 | She is bound to pass the exam. |
| boost one's confidence | 增强信心 | Success boosted his confidence. |
| be biased against | 对…有偏见 | The system is biased against the poor. |
| in brief | 简而言之 | In brief, the project was a success. |
| a broad range of | 广泛的 | The store offers a broad range of products. |
| burst into tears | 突然哭起来 | She burst into tears when she heard the news. |
| burst out laughing | 突然大笑 | Everyone burst out laughing at the joke. |
| on a budget | 预算有限 | We traveled on a tight budget. |
| be bound by | 受…约束 | We are bound by the terms of the contract. |
