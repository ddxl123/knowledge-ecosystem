# 高频词汇 List05 - 短语搭配

| 短语 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| commit oneself to | 致力于 | She committed herself to helping the poor. |
| commit a crime | 犯罪 | He committed a serious crime. |
| communicate with | 与…交流 | We need to communicate with each other. |
| compare...to... | 把…比作… | Life is often compared to a journey. |
| compare...with... | 把…与…比较 | Compare this product with that one. |
| be composed of | 由…组成 | The team is composed of experts. |
| concentrate on | 集中于 | Concentrate on one task at a time. |
| as far as...is concerned | 就…而言 | As far as I'm concerned, the plan is good. |
| be concerned about | 关心；担忧 | She is concerned about her health. |
| conclude with | 以…结束 | The meeting concluded with a summary. |
| come to a conclusion | 得出结论 | We came to the conclusion that more research is needed. |
| in conclusion | 总之 | In conclusion, we should take action now. |
| condemn sb. to | 判处某人 | He was condemned to life imprisonment. |
| conduct a survey | 进行调查 | We conducted a survey on consumer habits. |
| be confined to | 局限于 | The disease is confined to certain areas. |
| confirm sb. in | 使某人确信 | The results confirmed her in her belief. |
| come into conflict with | 与…发生冲突 | His actions came into conflict with the rules. |
| be confronted with | 面临 | We are confronted with many challenges. |
| be compulsory for | 对…来说是强制的 | The course is compulsory for all students. |
| reach a compromise | 达成妥协 | Both sides reached a compromise. |
