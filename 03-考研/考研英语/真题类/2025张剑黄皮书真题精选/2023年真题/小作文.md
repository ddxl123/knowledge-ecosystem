## 常见小作文类型 ⭐

| 类型 | 开头 | 结尾 |
|------|------|------|
| 建议信 | I am writing to offer some suggestions... | I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. |
| 邀请信 | I am writing to invite you to... | I look forward to your favorable reply. |
| 感谢信 | I am writing to express my gratitude... | Thank you again for your kindness. |

| 推荐信 | I am writing to recommend... | I am confident that... will be an excellent choice. |
| 申请信 | I am writing to apply for... | I would appreciate the opportunity to... |
| 通知 | Notice / Announcement | 发文单位 + 日期 |

## 2023年小作文精析

### 题目要求
（2023年小作文为通知：以学生会名义发布关于校园志愿者招募活动的通知。）

### 写作框架

**标题**：Notice

**正文第一段**：活动目的和背景
> In order to promote volunteer spirit and enhance students' social responsibility, the Student Union is recruiting volunteers for the upcoming community service program.

**正文第二段**：具体信息（时间、地点、要求）
> The program will run from March 15th to April 15th, 2023. Volunteers are expected to commit at least 4 hours per week. Activities include tutoring underprivileged children, visiting elderly homes, and organizing environmental campaigns. All students are welcome to apply regardless of major or year.

**正文第三段**：报名方式
> Interested students are requested to submit their applications to the Student Union office (Room 201, Student Center) by March 10th. For further information, please contact us at studentunion@university.edu.

**落款**：
> The Student Union
> March 1st, 2023

### 参考范文

> **Notice**
>
> In order to foster a spirit of volunteerism and enrich students' campus experience, the Student Union is pleased to announce a campus-wide volunteer recruitment drive for the Spring Community Service Program.
>
> The program, scheduled to run from March 15th to April 15th, will involve a variety of activities including environmental conservation, tutoring local schoolchildren, and assisting in community elderly care centers. Volunteers are expected to dedicate a minimum of four hours per week. No prior experience is required — only enthusiasm and commitment.
>
> All currently enrolled students are welcome to apply. Please submit your application form, available at the Student Union office (Room 201, Student Center), no later than March 10th. For inquiries, send an email to volunteer@university.edu.
>
> We look forward to your participation!
>
> The Student Union
> March 1st, 2023

## 评分要点与易错点 ⚠️

1. **格式**：通知要有标题 Notice，落款为单位+日期
2. **信息完整**：时间、地点、对象、联系方式缺一不可
3. **语言正式**：通知属于正式文体
4. **时间分配**：15-20 分钟
