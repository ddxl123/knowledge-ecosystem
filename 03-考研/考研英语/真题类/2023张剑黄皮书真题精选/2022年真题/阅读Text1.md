## 长难句分析

> *"Social media platforms, driven by algorithms designed to maximize user engagement, have inadvertently created echo chambers in which users are primarily exposed to information that reinforces their existing beliefs, thereby contributing to the growing polarization of public opinion."*

结构拆解：
- 主语：Social media platforms
- 插入语：driven by algorithms designed to maximize user engagement
- 主句谓语：have created
- 宾语：echo chambers
- in which 引导定语从句
- that reinforces... 为嵌套定语从句
- thereby contributing... 为结果状语

## 易错点 ⚠️

2. **推断题别推太远**——推理一步即可
