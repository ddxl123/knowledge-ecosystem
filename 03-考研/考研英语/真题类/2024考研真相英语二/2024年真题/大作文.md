## 解题技巧 ⭐

### 图表作文三段式

| 段落 | 内容 | 比例 |
|------|------|------|
| 第一段 | 描述图表数据，点明趋势 | 约 40 词 |
| 第二段 | 分析原因或解释现象 | 约 70 词 |
| 第三段 | 总结/预测/建议 | 约 40 词 |

### 常用描述词汇

| 图表类型 | 描述动词 | 示例表达 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 柱状图 | show, indicate, reveal | As is shown in the chart... |
| 折线图 | increase, decrease, fluctuate | The number rose sharply from... |
| 饼图 | account for, make up, constitute | A accounts for 30% of the total |
| 表格 | rank, compare, contrast | X ranks first among all categories |

### 数据描述万能句型
- The chart/graph/table shows that...
- As can be seen from the data...
- There is a significant increase/decrease in...
- The proportion of X is much higher/lower than that of Y.
