## 长难句分析

> *"The cascading effects of rising temperatures — from altered precipitation patterns that devastate crop yields in sub-Saharan Africa to the increased frequency of extreme weather events that disrupt supply chains — have created what many agricultural scientists describe as an unprecedented challenge to global food security."*

结构拆解：
- 主语：The cascading effects
- 破折号内为插入语，详细说明 effects 的内容
- that devastate / that disrupt 为定语从句
- what 引导宾语从句
