### 英语一经典三段式

**第一段：描述图画 + 揭示主题**（约 50 词）

**第三段：预测/建议**（约 40 词）

## 2023年大作文精析

### 题目要求
（2023年英语一大作文为图画作文：描绘了一位老人使用智能手机学习新技能的场景，旁边的年轻人在帮助老人。）

### 写作思路

**主题**：代际互助 / 终身学习 / 科技赋能

**第一段 — 描述图画**
> As is vividly depicted in the cartoon, an elderly woman is learning to use a smartphone with the patient guidance of a young girl sitting beside her. The warm scene conveys a touching message about intergenerational communication and the importance of lifelong learning in the digital age.

**第二段 — 分析原因/意义**
> The picture highlights two significant themes. First, it reflects the growing digital divide between generations, as rapid technological advancement has left many elderly people struggling to adapt. Second, it demonstrates the power of patience and empathy in bridging this gap. Young people, who are often digital natives, can play a crucial role in helping older generations embrace new technology. This intergenerational exchange benefits both parties: the elderly gain access to modern conveniences, while the young develop compassion and a sense of responsibility. Moreover, the scene embodies the spirit of lifelong learning — the belief that age should never be a barrier to acquiring new knowledge and skills.

**第三段 — 总结 + 建议**
> In conclusion, as technology continues to evolve at an unprecedented pace, it is imperative that we ensure no one is left behind. Families, communities, and governments should work together to create an inclusive digital environment. By fostering mutual understanding and support between generations, we can build a more harmonious and connected society.

| 维度 | 分析 |
|------|------|

## 高频句式模板

### 第一段（描述/引入）
- As is vividly depicted/shown in the picture/cartoon...
- The picture reveals/illustrates/reflects that...
- The warm/touching/striking scene conveys...

### 第二段（分析/论述）
- The picture highlights two significant themes.
- First... Second... Moreover...
- This phenomenon can be attributed to several factors.

### 第三段（总结/建议）
- In conclusion/To sum up/All things considered...
- It is imperative that...
- Only by... can we...

## 评分标准 ⭐

| 分数档 | 标准 |
|--------|------|
| 17-20 | 内容完整，语言流畅，句式多样，用词准确 |
| 13-16 | 内容较完整，语言基本准确，有一定句式变化 |
| 9-12 | 内容基本覆盖，有较多语言错误 |
| 5-8 | 内容不完整，语言错误较多 |
| 0-4 | 偏题或语言质量极差 |

## 易错点 ⚠️

1. **第一段只描述不评论**——分析放第二段
2. **不要用模板句堆砌**——要有自己的分析
3. **字数要够**——少于要求字数扣分
4. **避免低级语法错误**——主谓一致、时态、单复数
5. **书写工整**——卷面分很重要
6. **时间分配**：35-40 分钟
