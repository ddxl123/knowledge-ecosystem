### 英语一经典三段式

**第一段：描述图画 + 揭示主题**（约 50 词）
**第二段：分析原因/意义**（约 100 词）
**第三段：总结 + 建议/展望**（约 50 词）

**第二段：分析原因**（约 80 词）
**第三段：预测/建议**（约 40 词）

## 2024年大作文精析

### 题目要求
（2024年英语一大作文为图画作文：描绘了年轻人面对"躺平"与"奋斗"两种生活态度的选择。）

### 写作思路

**主题**：积极进取 vs 消极躺平

**第一段 — 描述图画**
> As is vividly depicted in the cartoon, two young people stand at a crossroads. One is climbing a steep mountain with determination, while the other is lying on a flat ground, seemingly content with doing nothing. The contrast between the two figures is both striking and thought-provoking.

**第二段 — 分析原因/意义**
> The picture reflects a growing social phenomenon among young people. Several factors contribute to this divergence in attitudes. First, the increasingly fierce competition in the job market has led some young people to feel overwhelmed and choose to withdraw. Second, the influence of social media has created a culture of instant gratification, discouraging long-term effort. However, those who persist in striving for their goals are more likely to achieve personal fulfillment and contribute to social progress. History has shown that perseverance and hard work remain the most reliable path to success.

**第三段 — 总结 + 建议**
> In my view, while it is understandable that some may feel exhausted by modern pressures, giving up entirely is not the solution. We should cultivate resilience, set realistic goals, and seek support when needed. Only by maintaining a positive and proactive attitude can we navigate the challenges of contemporary life.

### 高分范文亮点

| 维度 | 分析 |

## 高频句式模板

### 第一段（描述/引入）
- As is vividly depicted/shown in the picture/cartoon...
- The picture reveals/illustrates/reflects that...

### 第二段（分析/论述）
- Several factors contribute to this phenomenon.
- For example/instance...
- Moreover/Furthermore/What is more...

### 第三段（总结/建议）
- In conclusion/To sum up...
- It is advisable/suggested that...
- Only in this way can we...

## 易错点 ⚠️

1. **第一段只描述不评论**
2. **不要用模板句堆砌**——要有自己的分析
3. **字数要够**
4. **避免低级语法错误**
5. **时间分配**：35-40 分钟
