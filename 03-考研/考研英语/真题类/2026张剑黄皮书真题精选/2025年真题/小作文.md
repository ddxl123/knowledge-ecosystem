## 常见小作文类型 ⭐

### 1. 书信类

| 类型 | 开头 | 结尾 |
|------|------|------|
| 建议信 | I am writing to offer some suggestions... | I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. |
| 邀请信 | I am writing to invite you to... | I look forward to your favorable reply. |
| 感谢信 | I am writing to express my gratitude... | Thank you again for your kindness. |
| 道歉信 | I am writing to apologize for... | I sincerely hope you can accept my apology. |
| 投诉信 | I am writing to complain about... | I trust you will take this matter seriously. |
| 推荐信 | I am writing to recommend... | I am confident that... will be an excellent choice. |
| 申请信 | I am writing to apply for... | I would appreciate the opportunity to... |
| 通知 | Notice / Announcement | 发文单位 + 日期 |

### 2. 书信格式

```
Dear Sir/Madam,  （或具体称呼）

  第一段：说明写信目的
  第二段：具体内容
  第三段：期望回复/总结

Yours sincerely/faithfully,
（署名）
```

## 2026年小作文精析

### 题目要求
（2026年小作文为建议信：给学校图书馆写信，提出改善服务的建议。）

### 写作框架

**第一段**：自我介绍 + 写信目的
> I am a senior student who frequently uses the library. I am writing to offer some suggestions for improving library services.

**第二段**：具体建议（2-3 条）
> First, it would be beneficial to extend the opening hours during the exam period... Second, I suggest that the library provide more power outlets... Additionally, a mobile app for book reservations would greatly enhance efficiency...

**第三段**：总结 + 期望
> I hope you will consider these suggestions. I believe they would benefit both students and the library.

### 参考范文

> Dear Library Administrator,
>
> I am a frequent user of our university library and I am writing to offer several suggestions that could enhance the library's services.
>
> First, I would recommend extending the opening hours during the final examination period. Many students prefer studying late at night, and keeping the library open until midnight would greatly accommodate their needs. Second, it would be helpful to install more power outlets in the reading areas, as many students need to charge their laptops while studying. Finally, developing a mobile application for book reservations and renewals would streamline the borrowing process and reduce waiting times.
>
> I believe these improvements would significantly enhance the library experience for all students. Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

## 高频句式模板

| 功能 | 句式 |
|------|------|
| 开头 | I am writing to... / I would like to... |
| 建议 | I would suggest/recommend that... / It would be advisable to... |
| 理由 | This is because... / The reason is that... |
| 举例 | For example/instance... / A case in point is... |
| 总结 | In conclusion... / To sum up... |
| 期望 | I look forward to... / I would appreciate... |

## 评分要点与易错点 ⚠️

1. **格式不能错**：称呼、落款、分段必须正确
2. **字数别太少**：低于 80 词扣分，控制在 100-120 词
3. **不要漏要点**：题目要求的每个要点都要覆盖
4. **语言得体**：正式信函用正式语气，不要太口语化
5. **拼写和语法**：低级错误直接影响印象分
6. **时间分配**：小作文建议 15-20 分钟
